Beat 13, THE SHADE — one question, and it needs a look rather than a read

Four rungs, four failures on the same clause, and every mechanism that could fix it is now closed by measurement or contradicts canon. This page is the two frames and the one question. $0 — no render was made for it.

THE QUESTION. Does this satisfy "tips his head sideways into the sapling's hand-sized patch of shade" — or may the plant be drawn taller than canon in this one shot so the shade can actually reach his face?

Either answer closes the beat. "That's the shade, ship it" and it goes into the cut with the clause written off. "Draw it taller here" and the plate lane gets a one-shot exception to the 40 cm rule, written into canon as an exception rather than a drift. Or name a third thing.

1. Why it cannot be filmed as written — the measurement, on the beat's best frame

This is ep2-b13-shadelit-0820 at its last frame, the deepest head-tilt any rung has produced. He is seated on the ground; the sapling is at his hip. His eyes are about 320 px above the top of the two leaves, and the leaves themselves are about 60 px of plant. There is no head position in this staging that puts his face inside that shadow.

Measured off the frame, not asserted. The same reading appears twice in the code that draws this plate: beat13_shade_composite.py records that a canon 40 cm plant at his depth would crown at y 404 — his brow, "not what the approved line stages", and it carries a hard guard NO_DRAW_ABOVE_Y = 640 so nothing it draws can reach his face. A chest placement was tried and rejected by eye as "a flower pinned to his poncho".

2. This is the best the beat has ever looked, and that is the point

The right-hand frame is what an exposure rung bought this morning for $0 and no new words: same prompt, same seed, same mask, same anchor — one tone curve on the plate. It repaired a defect nobody had asked it to repair, the face going illegible in the middle of the shot.

So the question is not "can this be made prettier first". It is asked on the best pixels the beat owns, which is the only honest place to ask it.

3. What was already tried, so this is not a rerun of an old ask

  • Wording — three rungs. The middle-placement edit that repaired beats 03 and 15 gave this beat its performance back and did nothing for the shade.
  • Exposure — tested and exonerated. "Beat 13's plate is the dim one" is true of the field and false of the face: his cheek reads 114.6 against beat 03's 101.8 and beat 15's 93.3. He already had the brightest face in the episode.
  • Raising the plant — refused by the plate code itself, on canon: the sapling is about 40 cm and always shorter than he is, in every beat of 002b. That refusal is exactly what this question asks you to override, or not.
  • Lowering his head further — that is a new plate with a different pose. Authored work, not a derivation, and nobody should start it before the question above is answered.
  • The instrument — the measurement that was supposed to score the shade was retracted: it passes on the clip it was written to reject. Scored by eye instead, which is another reason this comes to you rather than to a number.

4. The line it is being judged against

The scavenger's legs give out and he drops to sit in the grass at the base of the stem, then tips his head sideways into the sapling's hand-sized patch of shade — the only part of him it will cover — knees up around his ears.

Your own earlier ruling is the other half of the tension: "thats ridiculous, lmao. the sapling is tiny." The line asks for shade on his face; the ruling makes the plant too small to cast it. Both are yours, so which one gives is yours too.
