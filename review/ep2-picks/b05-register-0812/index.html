The finding, and it is bigger than the guards

Look at the top row against the grid underneath it. Each frame reproduces the composition of the reference it was given, not the composition the beat asked for. The two references that are whole figures produced whole standing figures. The two that are head crops produced busts, floating heads and stacked head studies — no scene at all, on a beat about two men arriving in a field. Every one of the sixteen was given the same sentence.

The mechanism explains itself once you see it. The reference is fed to the image only during the first 6 of 40 denoising steps — deliberately, because that is where a picture's structure is decided and holding it there is what keeps the character's colour. But structure is also where pose, framing and how many people are in the shot are decided. So the reference settles those three before the sentence describing a skid ever gets a vote. A reference of a man standing still will keep producing a man standing still, whatever the words say and whatever the negative bans.

This also explains the wall the whole episode has been stuck behind. Nine of ten beats came back as standing character portraits instead of beats, and that was read as a wording problem for days. It was not. The references are standing portraits, and the conditioning copies the pose along with the face.

So your direction has not failed — it has not been tried

I predicted, before the run, that a failure could mean pose is simply not nameable the way objects are. That reading is wrong, and this lane already has the counter-example: on beat 03 a crouch that had been lost came back cleanly, from exactly this technique — make the pose the dominant clause and ban the pose it loses to. That worked at the motion stage, where there is no reference image overwriting the composition.

So the register is stageable. It is just not stageable here, in a still conditioned on a portrait. The cheap move is to stage it one stage later, in the clip, which is also where you already ruled the standing still is a usable base and where every object named in a prompt has arrived, 7 for 7.
