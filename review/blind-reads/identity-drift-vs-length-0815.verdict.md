**4. The softness of the IMAGE is the biggest limiter. ## Plain summary

- **HOLLOW — 4.** Reads as the same individual on skull, two-tone colouring, build and costume. The dissent is anatomical and specific: the ears are bigger, broader-based and rounder in outline than the reference's narrow blades, and the jaw is slightly wider. Framing moved closer and dropped toward eye level; sky went from blown white to blue. Open eyes are expression, not evidence.
- **PEWTER — 3.** Genuinely ambiguous. The clasped-hand pose and shirt are a direct match, but the eyes are a different organ — huge round staring orbs occupying far more face width than the reference's almond slits could — plus cheek blush, a rounder face and lower, more horizontal ears. Framing went from full body to a chest-up close-up at eye level, and the image is soft enough that fine features are partly unreadable, which is why I will not push this to 2 or to 4.
- **THISTLE — 5.** Same individual. Every landmark I can check — ear taper and angle, crown/face colour break, nose, mouth, chin, shoulder width, shirt cut and cuff roll, shorts hem, hand clasp — coincides. The only differences are open eyelids and a warmer, brighter grade with foliage shadow replacing sky. Framing is barely changed.

One cross-cutting caveat, stated once and applied to none of the ratings: all three IMAGEs carry a different colour grade from the reference, so I treated skin-tone differences as lighting unless the hue shift was accompanied by a shape change.
