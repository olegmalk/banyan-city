The one thing that needs you

Do the guards read as grown men — and if they do not, does that matter? On every one of these four beats the scoring lane wrote down the same thing without being asked to look for it: soft face, slight build, adolescent rather than adult. It is one question about the approved cast, not four separate faults.

Three ways out, and all three are yours. One word is a complete answer — the work each one sets off is already written down and waiting, so nothing comes back to you for a second decision:

  • "pass" — they read grown enough; accept them as they are. Fires immediately: beat 09's clip goes into the cut. Every step is pre-written in pipeline/decisions-pending/ep2-guards-0818/ and needs no further call from you.
  • "recast" — keep drawing until they read grown, and treat anything younger as a miss. Beat 09 stays a slate; a casting rung gets filed for the four beats.
  • "stage" — crop, angle and coverage carry the age instead of the faces. This is the option beat 09's own close-up is evidence for: it scored 5 of 12 adult where the wide shots scored 0 of 12.

What is not on the menu: writing adult into the prompt. Your 2026-08-12 ruling — "chase silly via pose and expression staging" — closed the adjective route after five rounds of body-type and tone words (round, soft bodies, harmless) drew solemn men 0 of 4 every time. Body-type and tone words stay out of the prompt and the register gets staged. That ruling covers this word too, so "just say adult" is not an option I can offer you.

And a second one, smaller

May the goblin read as a plain green man? On the beats where he shares a frame with a guard, his pointed ears and patched cloak survive 1 of 12 — everywhere else he is a smooth green figure in the same robe as the guard. That is not only a wording miss: bald turns out to be broadcast-class, so naming his bare scalp puts it on every human head in frame. On any beat that also holds a human, his goblin-ness cannot be named in words at all — it has to come from a reference or from staging.

So the call is whether a plain green man is acceptable, or whether he must read as a goblin and the beats that pair him with a guard get built differently to protect it.
