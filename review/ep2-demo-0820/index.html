WHY THIS PAGE MEASURES THE FINISHED FILE INSTEAD OF THE TAKES — the trap that would have shipped the old voices a second time.

The re-voice record and the work ladder both say the assembler picks these takes up out of the node directory automatically: "no flag, no copy step." The code disagrees. find_audio() and vo_manifest() in render_t3.py resolve against the --clips directory and nowhere else — there is no fallback to the node — so running the documented command verbatim would have re-muxed the old VO into a page announcing new voices. Pointing --clips at the node's own folder is not a way out either; that directory holds no clips and the tool refuses it.

So the six mp3 and their six json sidecars were copied in explicitly. And because a hash of a copied file only proves what went into the directory, the finished mp4 was measured two independent ways:

  • Waveform. Each beat's audio was decoded back out of the episode and cross-correlated against the new take and the old take. Against the new take: 0.975, 0.986, 0.983, 0.994, 0.993, 0.987. Against the old take: 0.062, 0.047, 0.073, 0.071, 0.084, 0.149. Six for six, and not close.
  • Pitch. The table above — measured on audio pulled out of each episode with the same estimator over the same window, so the two cuts are comparable.

Both numbers for Guard 2's separation are honest and they differ: 26.0 Hz measured on the bare mp3 takes, 32.3 Hz measured through the master over the whole beat including the wind bed. Different windows, different numbers, both well clear of the 20 Hz bar.

Two limits that travel with the re-voice, from its own record rather than discovered here. Short lines undershoot: beat 07 is five words and rendered 177.8, 174.5, 170.2 and 181.8 across four attempts, consistently below the same voice's long lines — the clone does not get enough voiced material to reach its reference. The best of the four is what is in the cut. And Guard 1 and the scavenger overlap per take even though their medians are 24.2 Hz apart, because the scavenger's own takes span a 36 Hz range that predates this pass. Not fixed: he is outside your ruling's scope, and the mitigating fact is that the two never alternate — the guards speak beats 05–10 and the scavenger 13–20 — whereas the pair you actually caught alternates line by line.

The palindrome fix — its first published use

When a beat's slot outran its clip by more than 0.05s, render_t3 filled the gap by playing the footage forward and then reversed, looping that palindrome — and it printed nothing while doing it. In 0819c it was firing on 8 of the 18 footage beats: 1, 3, 4, 6, 10, 11, 17 and 18. The clip now plays once and its final frame is held for the remainder, and the tool prints the substitution on every beat it fires on. The reversal survives only as an opt-in flag that nothing in this tree sets.

MEASURED, ON TWO BEATS, NOT INFERRED FROM THE COMMIT. Frames were pulled from the assembled cuts at 24fps across each beat's fill window — the span after its clip has already played once — and cropped to the picture strictly above the caption strip, because caption text changes on voice-chunk boundaries and that is motion which is not the footage.

  • Beat 01 — a 5.04s clip in a 9.46s slot, identical in both cuts. Moving frame-pairs in the fill window: 105 of 105 in 0819c → 0 of 105 here. 70 of those pairs are now bit-identical; the largest single-pixel change anywhere is 4 levels, which is codec ringing.
  • Beat 06 — a 1.92s clip in a 6.46s slot. 100 of 114 → 0 of 108, 80 pairs bit-identical.

And what the fill now contains, rather than just that it is still: every fill frame was matched against every frame of its own clip. In 0819c beat 01's best match walks backwards — 120, 119, 118, 117, 116 … — and beat 06's runs 45 → 0 → 45 → 25, two direction changes inside one slot, which is the board turning backwards, forwards and backwards again. In this cut it is the last frame, every time: frame 120 for all 105 of beat 01's fill frames and frame 45 for all 109 of beat 06's.

The other five were not frame-checked. Beats 3, 4, 11, 17 and 18 take the same branch and print the same line, but two beats were verified and five are inferred. Said plainly rather than implied.

Beat 01 is the subtle one and it is included for honesty rather than for drama. The fig is already purple early on, so the reversal shows mainly at the fig's crown and in the drifting specks — which is why the numbers above, not this picture, are the evidence.

A claim from 0819c that the pixels do not support, corrected rather than repeated. That page and its manifest both said beat 01's fig "goes green, ripens to purple, and un-ripens to green again". Opening the frames, the fig is purple by 1.2s and stays purple. The reversal is real and it is 105 pairs out of 105 — the colour story wrapped around it was overstated.

An honest cost of the fix, on the beat with the biggest gap. Beat 18 is a 5.04s clip in an 10.96s slot, so it now holds a still frame for 5.9 seconds. It previously ran a tremble backwards — and a tremble reversed still reads as a tremble. This is the one beat where the old behaviour was arguably less noticeable than the new one. The real cause is unchanged either way: the voice is longer than the footage, and the fix for that is longer footage or shorter lines, per beat.

Beat 10 quietly dropped off the list, and it was the re-voice that did it — not a second fix. Its new take is 0.10s shorter, which pulled the slot to 4.08s against a 4.04s clip, and a 0.04s margin is inside the branch's own threshold. So the fill fires on seven beats here, not eight. Side effect, recorded so nobody reads it as an achievement.

The runtime moved by 0.13 seconds, and the voice got 1.77 seconds longer

Both are true and they look contradictory, so here is the arithmetic. Slots fit to the longer of the script length and the voice length, so voice growth underneath the script floor buys no runtime at all: beat 05 gained 0.86s of speech and 0.04s of slot; beat 07 gained 1.11s of speech and 0.21s of slot; beat 09 gained 0.18s of speech and nothing. Meanwhile beats 06, 08 and 10 got shorter and gave slot back — −0.25s, −0.05s, −0.09s. Net −0.14s of slot, against −0.13s measured. No clip was stretched, trimmed, looped or reversed.

A correction while we are here: 0819c's own manifest records its duration as 113.29s. The published file measures 113.46s. The delta above is against the measurement, not against that number.
