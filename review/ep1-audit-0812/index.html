Provenance and licence — the two things that block publishing, regardless of taste

1. The canon stills carry no machine-readable provenance

I ran the licence gate's own resolver over the approved stills directory: 0 of 29 files have a sidecar it can find. Their provenance exists — it is written in prose in that folder's README, and it is in git history — but no tool can read it, which means no tool can check it. The working takes/ folder next door has 113 sidecars, so this is specific to the promoted, canonical set: the files that matter most are the ones with the least machine-readable history. The repair I was told to check for has not landed.

2. The cut is currently unpublishable, and it is not about this cut

Assembling v42 ran 26 ingredients through the licence gate and 4 were refused — the stills under beats 06, 09, 12 and 15, all on the same ground: their model licence (CreativeML Open RAIL++-M) has never been cleared for redistribution under our own CC BY offer. This is the standing D15 debt, not a new fault, and it is the same for every version of this episode. It does not affect screening. It does mean that "ready to be published", the third of your three gates for finished, is not currently satisfied by anything.
