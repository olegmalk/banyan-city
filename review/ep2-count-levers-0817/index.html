Beat 01 — three ways to ask for two leaves, and none of them worked

2026-08-17, overnight. 48 frames, three prompt rungs, each scored against a bar written before its frames existed. Nothing here is promoted and nothing is cut. One question at the bottom is yours; the rest is measurement and it is already decided.

The one thing I need from you

Rows r2 and r3 keep producing a bush-sized purple fig with two little leaves perched on top. Several are genuinely handsome pictures. They also fail this beat's own bar, which asks for a seedling no taller than the grass around it — the script's cold open is a tiny sapling in a field, and these are a fruit the size of a shrub.

Do you want that look? Three ways to answer:

  • Hold the bar — the seedling stays small, and I stop showing you the big-fig frames. Beat 01 gets built by compositing (see below).
  • Change the beat — you like the giant fig, and the cold open becomes that instead. This is a script change and it is yours, not mine.
  • Name a frame — if one of these is the shot, say which and I will build from it.

I am not asking which of the 48 is best — picking takes is mine and I will do it. I am asking whether the bar itself is still what you want, because I keep failing frames against it that you might actually like.

What was tested, and what happened

The cold open asks for a two-leaf sapling. The model will not draw two leaves — it draws four to eight, on two or three stacked nodes. Three different ways of asking, one variable each, all against the same control and the same 16-cell grid:

The finding that closes the question

These two sheets are different wordings. Look at cell r2-s3 in each — the fig with two leaves on top — and r3-s1:

Three different prompts did not each independently invent that composition. The reference images are drawing it, not the words. Which means the ~3-of-16 is the floor of this setup, and every wording we have measured has been measuring the reference set.

So the wording route is closed, and I am not spending the card on it again

Five wordings have now been measured on this grid: exactly two 0 of 16, the canon draft 0 of 16, opposed one either side of the stem 2 of 16, then tonight's three at 3 of 16. That is not five near misses, it is one null measured five times.

The route that already works is compositing: build the two leaves into the starting image and let the model paint over it at low strength. That gives an exact two-leaf count 8 times out of 8, and it spends no GPU on the count at all. The next beat-01 job should produce a plate that way rather than test another sentence.

What is still broken and nobody has worked on

Height, 0 of 16 on every rung including the control. Every frame is shot too close: the grass is out of shot or reduced to a strip, so “no taller than the grass around it” has nothing to measure against. This is a framing problem, no count wording touches it, and it is the more shippable of the two defects. It has never had a rung of its own.
