How these were made, and why it matters

A queue, not a person. mac_worker.py drains a job directory on each Mac under caffeinate; mac_enqueue.py --spread files across whichever workers are alive. Ten jobs, zero new failures, drained unattended.

This matters because every idle stretch in this project has had one cause: the machines were fine, the work existed, and nothing handed one to the other. The 5090 idled eleven hours on 08-17; these Macs idled twenty-one. Both were hand-driven.
