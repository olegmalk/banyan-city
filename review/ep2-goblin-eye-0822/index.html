The eye — is this your goblin now?

You said “that aint my goblin” of the sweep. It was the eye, and it took four rounds on beat 13 to find the two words that fix it. Every plate in the episode has been re-rendered on the result. One question at the bottom.

What was wrong

Your picture draws a small off-white almond with a tiny dark pupil, low and wide-set in a very large dome — the face is mostly forehead. Every frame we were making drew a big rendered iris in a white sclera. Side by side at the same scale they are two different characters, which is what you saw at a glance.

Why the obvious fixes did nothing

The prompt had been naming the pupil — slit pupils, constricted pupils — and a rule in our own code was requiring it on every render. On this model that tag draws a large iris with a slit through it, which is exactly the thing you rejected. Negating it did not help either; the negative was also banning blank eyes, which is very nearly what your eye actually is.

What worked was not a feature word at all. Two tags that describe how an eye is drawn rather than what it looks like:

tagwhat it changed
eyebagsthe FILL — iris becomes an off-white field with a dark pupil
jitomethe SIZE and shape — the flat, deadpan, narrow eye
large eyes, big eyes (negative)holds the scale down
