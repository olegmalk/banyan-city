Beat 01 — the new cold open, and the one thing the cut does to it

The clip passed a bar that was written before it existed. Seed 20260826 of ep2-b01-growmotion-b-0818, on the frame-1 plate that passed its own bar 7 of 7:

  • G2 end state — PASS. Deep purple-violet.
  • G3 two leaves — PASS. Two blades in every frame read.
  • G4 scale — PASS. No balloon; the fig stays below the grass tops.
  • G5 camera — PASS. Whole-frame best fit f000→f120 is zoom 1.05 with a 4-unit shift, the tightest lock of the six seeds.

Its one stated caveat, restated here rather than left on the other page: the light blooms hard between the first and second seconds and keeps rising, so by the end the sky has washed from dawn purple to pale amber with wide yellow streaks. That is a change in the LIGHT, not a loss of the DRAWING — every grass blade is still a drawn blade in the plate's own hand — which is why it is a caveat and not a fail.

The new fault, and it belongs to the CUT

The clip is 5.0s. Beat 01's slot in this cut is 9.5s, because the slot is sized to the VO and beat 01's VO is nine seconds long. When footage is shorter than its slot the pipeline fills it by palindrome — the clip forward, then the clip reversed — so that every seam is motion-continuous. On almost every beat that is invisible. On a shot whose entire subject is a fig growing, it means the last half-second plays the growth backwards.

You can see it below: six frames from 7.5s to 9.5s. The fig goes purple → mauve → mint, under the caption Total assets: two leaves and one fig.

Not fixed, and the reason is that every fix is somebody else's call. Shortening the slot means cutting the VO. Filling it means a longer clip, which is a render. Holding the last frame instead of reversing is a pipeline change that would apply to every beat in every episode, and it is a change I would want you to see argued before it shipped. The incumbent ep2-b01-purple filled the slot natively at 9.7s and had no such tail — if the tail bothers you more than the old cold open did, say so and the old one goes back in. The two clips are not variants of each other: different plate, different scale, different palette, so this is a swap, not a tweak.

Beat 14 — footage at last, and it misses its bar

Beat 14 has been a slate on every demo cut. On 2026-08-18 its plate was ruled shipped and one seed was animated off it. One sample, one seed — no sweep was fired.

VERDICT: FAIL, on M2 and M3 — but not for the reason the spec predicted, and that is the interesting part.

  • M1 — hands to the dirt — PASS. The hands travel from clasped on his knees at f000 to planted in the ground by f105, and the ground is genuinely disturbed: unbroken green grass at f000, exposed dark brown soil under both hands with his claw tips sunk into it by f121.
  • M2 — the glance — FAIL. His head does lift, from fully bowed to fully raised. But it never comes back and it never turns to the side. The bar asked for a distinguishable look away and return; this is a single monotonic lift, which is the drift the bar excluded by name.
  • M3 — embarrassment — FAIL. With the face up from f105, the expression is flat and half-lidded and reads sullen, not caught-out. This is the second time that read has appeared on this beat — it was already recorded against a different plate seed — so it is a property of how the beat is staged, not of one unlucky picture.
  • M4 — same figure, locked camera — PASS. One adult goblin throughout, no reframe, both knees down for all 121 frames, and best-fit centre zoom f000→f120 measures ×1.00 at 0.01 granularity.
  • The standing pale-limb check — PASS. No pale limb-like mass in any frame. The large cream oval at lower left is his own knee in pale grey trousers, present in the plate at that position and still a knee at f121.

What the failure is actually worth, because it is worth something

The spec predicted frozen hands, on the strength of in-hand actions measuring 0 of 8 on this engine against whole-body actions at 12 of 12. The hands were not frozen. They reached, planted and broke ground. What that shows is that beat 14's action decomposes, and the two halves behave exactly as the 0-of-8 / 12-of-12 split predicts once you tell them apart: reaching an arm down to the ground is gross whole-body motion and it rendered; picking repeatedly at dirt with the fingers is fine in-hand action and it did not — the hands arrive and then hold. The gap to done_when is now two named clauses, not the whole shot.

Why it is not trimmed, since its own verdict says it should be

The verdict's note to the beat's owner says 80 of the 121 frames are frozen and a trim to the f085-f121 window is the obvious first edit. It is not trimmed here, for a mechanical reason rather than a taste one: that window is 1.5s and beat 14's slot in this cut is 5.1s, so a trimmed clip would be looped or palindromed to fill it — the same mechanism that put the backwards tail on beat 01, and worse than the frozen head it removes. The full take is in and its frozen two-thirds is visible. In the strip above, the first five frames are the frozen part.
