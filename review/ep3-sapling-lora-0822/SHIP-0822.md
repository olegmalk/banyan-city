# The sapling LoRA — trained, measured, and not shippable yet

**2026-08-22, overnight. $0. One card-hour on an otherwise idle box.**
Steward's measurement. **Nothing here is a taste call and nothing has been
published** — every frame carries `approved: false` / `provisional: true`.

---

## The one line

**`bnysapling` draws the canon two-leaf sapling.** The checkpoint has never once
drawn it from words — `pipeline/work-ladder-0819.md` records five closed wording
ladders, all failing the same way: bead-strung vines, faceted crystals on bare
twigs, leaf counts of 4 and 8 and 3 but never 2. A three-token trigger now
returns it, **14 times out of 15 on the committed count ruler**, in the show's
line and palette.

**Three of the five pre-registered bars still fail**, and all three failures
trace to the same two facts about the dataset: **44 of 44 training frames are
figure-free, and 44 of 44 stand on grass.** That is a variety problem, not a
hyperparameter problem, and it is fixable with GPU time we already have.

The cold reader was blind in the way the bar specifies — a fresh agent given one
frame and the canon clause from `THE-SAPLING.md`, no prompt text, no context, no

**B5 passing is worth more than it looks.** The dataset's own negative
(`three leaves, four leaves, many leaves, extra stalk, branching stem…`) was
deliberately kept OUT of every prompt here, so nothing suppressed extra blades on
the LoRA's behalf. Fourteen of fifteen frames drew exactly two because the
weights carry the count. *(The Jerry spec put `glasses` in its sample negative
and then made "zero of 15 wear glasses" a bar — a bar its own negative answers.
That circularity is not repeated here.)*

Reported alongside, per the committed ruler: **shape** — ordinary average blades
throughout, no round cotyledon buttons, no fig lobes, so the founder's 2026-08-17
shape ruling held. **Height** — the plant sits at or below the grass line in
essentially every frame, which is the same observation as the scale failure read
from the other side.

## The three failures are one root cause

**Every frame in the training set is a plant alone on grass.** Both monocultures
were named in `pipeline/lora/README.md` before training. Both showed up exactly
where they were predicted to.

**1 — No figure in 44 of 44 frames, so the LoRA has learned "no figure".** This
is the biggest one and it reaches three separate bars:

- Asked for *a goblin crouching beside it*: **0 of 3.** One seed drew a creature
  with the plant fused into its body — the blind reader, with no idea what it was
  looking at, wrote *"Creature body with face, limbs"*. The other two drew no
  figure at all.
- Two of B1's four identity misses are that same fusion: a sapling growing out of
  a dark lump.
- In the no-regression pair, the small figure standing on the bridge is present
  without the LoRA and **gone with it**. An object deleted is not a tone shift.

**2 — Grass in 44 of 44 frames, so the trigger generalises ground COLOUR but not
ground MATERIAL.** Asked for *dry autumn grassland, brown grass*: **3 of 3**,
clean — the palette is not baked. Asked for *bare tilled earth field*: **1 of 3**;
the other two returned a green meadow. Different colour is fine, different
substance is not.

**3 — Story height 15/25/40 cm only, so 90 cm does not draw.** All three frames
put the plant at the same apparent height against the grass line as the 15 cm
frames. The height token did not stay promptable, which is the failure
`research §8.5` predicted in advance. **It matters for ep4 and later, not ep3** —
003b's sapling is still small.

## The weight ladder runs backwards, and this is the useful surprise

`CONTACT-ladder-subject-0822.png`. When B3 failed at 0.8 the obvious move was to
turn the LoRA down, so the ladder measured 0.65 / 0.50 / 0.35 / 0.20 on both
sides at one seed.

- **Clean side** (no trigger): contamination fades smoothly as the weight drops.
  At 0.20 the frame is close to the no-LoRA frame in light, framing and bridge
  position.
- **Subject side**: the sapling **collapses** below 0.65. At 0.50 it sprouts from
  a green bulb with a face; at 0.35 it grows extra leaves; at 0.20 it is a round
  green mascot creature with closed eyes and two leaves on its head.

That is the base checkpoint's own prior coming back — the exact creature the five
closed wording ladders kept returning. **The LoRA at high weight is the thing
suppressing it.** So the two curves never cross: the usable band is 0.65–0.8, and
B3 fails across all of it. The ladder's own pre-registered success line said *"a
ladder with no crossing point is a full result"*, and that is the result.

**No shipping weight is sanctioned.** `pipeline/lora/registry.yaml` records
`shipping_weight: null`.

## Recorded, not graded: leaf count is not promptable

Declared in advance as a measurement with no pass or fail attached, because the
set could not teach it — `leaf_count_values: 1`, every training frame two-leaf.

**Asked for four leaves, the trigger drew two.** The count is fused into the
trigger.

That is fine for ep3 and it is a wall after it. Canon rises 2 → 6 leaves by 006a
(`genomes/sapling/style.md:150-158`), and this LoRA cannot follow. **The v2 rung —
a compositor that can draw three, five and six blades — was previously "named and
not written". It is now the thing that gates every episode after 003b.**

The ban is LTX-scoped by its own words, and it is scoped that way because its

**Zero LTX pixels entered this run, and that is enforced in code rather than
promised.** Every one of the 44 frames is: an SDXL text plate (animagine-xl-3.1)
→ a numpy composite → one 0.30 SDXL inpaint. The training job's stage step
refuses to proceed unless each frame's sha256 matches the manifest **and** the
manifest asserts an animagine chain for that exact frame. It printed
`staged 44 image+caption pairs` with zero mismatches.

**The steward is not closing item 18.** What is argued is that this run falls
outside its scope. The ruling is the founder's, and it now has pixels attached.

## What it cost, and what broke on the way

**$0. No provider, no spend, no leaf touched, no canon changed, no publish.**
Training: 660 optimizer steps in **20m02s**, final avr_loss 0.105.

Three technical failures, all found and fixed the same night:

1. **`min_bucket_reso 1024` asserts against an 832 short side.** sd-scripts
   refuses `min(resolution) >= min_bucket_reso` before loading a weight. The
   1024/2048 pair comes from research §5's standard-SDXL config, written for
   1024² training where it passes by coincidence. One number: 1024 → 832.
   **`train-jerry-0820.yaml` carries the identical pair and would die the same
   way in its own first fifteen seconds.**
2. **`sample_lora.py` had never been run.** diffusers 0.29.2 gates all LoRA
   loading behind `USE_PEFT_BACKEND`, and `peft` was absent from both venvs. The
   job trained cleanly for twenty minutes and then died in nine seconds. Fixed
   with `pip install --no-deps peft==0.12.0` (freeze diff: exactly one line;
   torch unchanged at 2.11.0+cu128; bf16 matmul on the card re-verified). The
   sampler now refuses up front with that command in the message.
3. **A rank-32 SDXL checkpoint is 228 MB, not the ~35 MB research §5 claims** —
   that is the SD1.5 number carried forward, wrong by 6.5×, and five of them
   were about to be copied into `farm-out/`. GitHub hard-rejects any blob over
   100 MiB, and the courier commits `farm-out` locally before pushing, so **one
   of these would have left a permanently unpushable commit and stopped every
   other lane's results reaching the tree.** Caught before the publish step ran.
   Now blocked in three places, each verified with `git check-ignore`. Weights
   stay on the box; their sha256s travel.

## The ladder from here — cheapest first, none of it needs the founder awake

1. **A figure batch. ~40 GPU-minutes, and it is the one that matters.** Composite
   the canon sapling onto plates that CONTAIN A GOBLIN. Three bars fail on this
   one gap and ep3's entire on-screen cast is these two characters, so a sapling
   LoRA that erases the other half of the cast cannot serve the episode it was
   built for.
2. **A non-grass ground batch. ~20 GPU-minutes.** Tilled earth, stone, sand,
   snow, floorboards. Colour already generalises; material does not.
3. **Grade the earlier epochs.** Four checkpoints at epochs 2/4/6/8 are on the
   box unexamined. Overfitting past the sweet spot is the standard failure and
   the fusion defect is what overfitting to "plant alone" looks like — an earlier
   epoch may hold the sapling with less of it. ~15 GPU-minutes per grid.
4. **The leaf-count compositor.** Now urgent, per the probe above.
5. **The plate-step loader is deliberately NOT written.** `render_wave_sample.py`,
   `still_local.py` and `controlnet_plate.py` contain no LoRA code at all; the
   change is about eight lines at `render_wave_sample.py:236` plus the sidecar
   fields. The standing rule is no work without a consumer, and the consumer for
   a loader is a LoRA that passed. This one has not.
