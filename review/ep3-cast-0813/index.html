Episode 3 path — the cast that has never been drawn

Three people carry most of episodes 4 to 7 and not one of them has ever been rendered: the farmer, the assessor and the magistrate. These are first candidates, four seeds each, drawn from the scripts’ own wording. Nothing here is cast, nothing is canon, and the verdict is yours. Two are below. The third is not, and the reason is at the bottom — please read that part.

The farmer

004’s second citizen, and in every episode after it. ep3-charref-farmer-r2-0812

“a broad squarish silhouette in a straw hat, three lines for his whole face, permanently unimpressed” — 005’s own continuity block
What the script asked for and got Straw hat, arms crossed, an unimpressed face. He reads as a farmer.
What is offHe is slim where the script says broad and squarish, and he is standing under a wooden veranda rather than on plain ground — a reference wants nothing behind it.

The assessor

In fifteen of episode 5’s twenty-six beats. ep3-charref-assessor-r3-0812

“a thin vertical line of a man in dust-grey robes, ruler-straight edges, a heavy ledger chained to his belt, a quill worn to a stub; he moves like a metronome” — 005’s own continuity block
WorkingThin, tall and vertical, quill in hand, a chain at the belt — and he is the same man across all four seeds, which is the property a reference actually needs.
He is bald, and nobody asked for that The prompt never says bald. The checkpoint did it on its own, in all four. You said this two days ago about the guards — “why are you making everyone bald..?” — so it is flagged rather than quietly shipped a second time.
Two more things off The ledger is missing, which is his defining prop — there is a chain but no book on the end of it. And the low camera and storm light make him look menacing, which fights R2 and 005’s own note that he is “diligence, not menace — a man whose forms cannot hold the world he is honestly trying to record.” The robes also came out pale green rather than dust-grey.

The magistrate — withheld

In twenty-one of episode 6A’s thirty-one beats. ep3-charref-magistrate-r2-0812

“tall in travel-worn robes of office with a heavy seal on a chain — she never hurries and never pauses at a threshold” — 006a’s own continuity block
I am not putting these in front of you She rendered, and what came back is not a magistrate. She is the first woman this show has ever tried to draw, and the count tag the pipeline requires for a female character (1girl) carries this checkpoint’s anime-pinup prior with it: both seeds I examined came back as a sexualised bust — open low-cut robe, exaggerated chest, coy blush — with no seal, no ledger, no office and nothing of a woman who never hurries.

It is not a near miss and it is not one bad seed, so I did not put the frames on this page and I did not spend another round guessing at it. The frames exist on the results branch if you want to see what I am describing; they are not published anywhere.

What it means beyond her: every future female character hits the same wall. Fixing it needs explicit wardrobe language and a negative tier the pipeline does not currently have — a recipe decision worth making deliberately, in daylight, rather than at one in the morning by me.

Four seeds each, $0, local GPU, nothing published and nothing promoted. Provenance sidecars were written at render time (§7.2) and every frame here was hash-verified against the box’s own manifest before it was looked at. These are candidates for your eye — no ranking, no recommendation, and nothing is cast until you point at a frame.

The sixty-odd beats across episodes 4 to 7 that need these three people stay unqueued until then, which is the same rule you set for the guards.
