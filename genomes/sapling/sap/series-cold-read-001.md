# Series cold read — all seven trunk episodes, one stranger, in order

**2026-07-25.** A context-free reader was given the `## Script` of 001, 002b,
003b, 004, 005, 006a and 007a in order, plus `style.md`, and forbidden every
other file — no shot lists, no sap, no specs, no prior transcripts. The brief
was to find what a per-episode review cannot see.

This is the first time the series has been read as a series. It is the most
useful document in `sap/`, and most of what it found is **not** a bug in the
pipeline — it is the shape of the show. Those parts are the author's call (R4)
and are recorded here rather than acted on.

## The good news, and it is real

The reader retold all seven episodes accurately and unaided, from the death at
the desk to the pilgrim kneeling at dawn. Whatever else is wrong, **the story
is followable end to end.** It also named the series' strongest structural
idea without being prompted: the ladder of visitors — patrol → farmer →
assessor → magistrate → pilgrim — each wanting something harder to formalise
than the last.

Three things it could not tell us after seven episodes: *what the tree wants,
how big it is at any point, and a single character's name.*

## Continuity faults (fixable, mostly factual)

1. **001's hook is contradicted by 002b's first frame.** 001 ends on heavy
   footsteps arriving *now*; 002b opens with the tree grown from a 15 cm
   two-leaf sprout to 40 cm with a side branch and a ripe fig. Weeks passed
   across a smash cut. Also: the footsteps are *heavy* and what arrives is a
   small round goblin.
2. **003b → 004 splits one sentence across a day.** 003b ends at dusk on "I
   hereby name this place—"; 004 resumes the line, then plays in midday sun.
   The tree also jumps from two leaves and a thin branch to knee-high with a
   sittable shade patch.
3. **A third capability appears as an aside.** 001 is built on "sense and grow
   — that's the whole API", with *move* ruled out on screen. 004's VO says "I
   have been moving water toward his field for three days." Never discovered,
   never costed, and there is no room for three days in the timeline.
4. **The tree's height contradicts the growth ladder and itself.** 004 and 005
   both say knee-high one day apart; 006a opens the next morning on "the height
   of a man". `style.md` puts 005 at 1.2 m while 005's own script says knee-high
   twice. No animator can serve both.
5. **The cost of a leaf-tilt is established as everything he has, then becomes
   free.** 003b's engine is that one tilt costs the day's whole reserve — the
   last teal glow goes out. 007a has him rehearsing multi-branch choreography
   on cue for two weeks *while banking* two weeks of growth. The show's only
   physical constraint quietly disappears.
6. **The growth arithmetic doesn't close.** 001: three days of full effort buys
   one leaf. 007a: fourteen days buys an entire fruiting crown in eight seconds.
7. **The one deadline in the series is broken two beats later.** 004's hook is
   "after harvest, the assessor counts everything with a name"; 005 opens next
   dawn with the assessor arriving, and lampshades it. The joke lands and costs
   the hook all of its pressure.
8. **Three consecutive episodes end announcing an arrival and open with that
   arrival already present.** Nothing is ever *awaited*.
9. **Population is counted three different ways in three episodes** (1, then 2
   with the tree counted, then "two" and "three" twenty seconds apart in 005).
   The farmer is also written into Shade's occupations while living on his own
   field, having said "tell no one".
10. **The wireframe/terminal overlay is used twice and abandoned.** `$ whoami /
    sapling (ficus. probably.)` and 004's single-frame wireframe label are the
    most original thing in the visual grammar; after 004 status is only a
    non-diegetic end card.
11. **The patrol guards are the funniest thing in the series and never return**
    — and their unfileable apple warrant is closed out by a different character.
12. **006a's final image is a hook 007a ignores.** 006a ends on the magistrate:
    "shrines declare *to whom*. Those words outlive towns", over a close-up of
    the empty ruled line. 007a never mentions it.
13. **The fig relic does unlicensed work.** Returned in 003b, denied in 005
    ("Zero fruit. Historically."), produced from his cloak in 006a, and touched
    in 007a "where the fig once hit him". Nothing establishes he kept it.
14. **The magistrate warms up offscreen** — pure jurisprudence in 006a, arriving
    early with a folding chair in 007a.
15. **"I can't even wave" (002b) is never revisited** once the protocol makes
    him articulate.
16. **Nothing from his human life ever returns.** 001 spends 25 seconds on the
    desk, the retry loop and the shattering mug; afterwards his old existence is
    only a supply of tech metaphors. He misses no one, nobody is looking for
    him, there is no unfinished thing.

## The three findings that are the author's call (R4)

These are not defects to patch. They are decisions about what the show is, and
they are reserved.

**The protagonist has no want, and is not driving his own series.** Episode by
episode he wants to finish an inventory, to answer, to keep the goblin, to be
understood — then for three episodes he wants nothing; he is asked questions and
consents. His climactic act of will in 006a is signing a form. *The active want
from 003b to 006a belongs entirely to the goblin: a legal place to exist.* The
reader's conclusion is that the goblin is the actual protagonist and the tree is
his narrator — and that 007a's "He said the fig was the whole tree. Fine. Then
here's the whole tree" is the only moment in seven episodes where the tree makes
a costly decision alone, and the only moment the voice drops its irony.

**Nobody has a name — and the only name in the series belongs to a walk-on.**
"Dren" is given to a patrol guard in episode 2 who never returns. So namelessness
is not a rule, it is an omission: the series is willing to name people, it just
never names anyone who matters. The cost is concrete — a viewer cannot discuss
the show without saying "the goblin", which flattens the character it works
hardest on, and 006a's payload (*"I've never been a line item before"*) is
undercut because we have never had anything to call him but his species. The
reader's proposed fix is the sharpest thing in the report: **have the magistrate
read the keeper's name off the form aloud in 006a** — that is the beat where he
becomes a line item, and a line item should have a name on it.

**004, 005 and 006a are the same episode three times.** An official with a
document enters the clearing, asks questions, the tree tilts a leaf, the official
writes it down, a monospace card updates, smash to black. And 002b/003b/004 run a
second triple whose mechanic is "goblin asks, leaf tilts". Related: **four
episodes open with the literally identical sentence** — "I died an engineer and
woke up as this tree" — and the other three open with variants. Seven recaps,
four word-for-word.

Also structural, and connected: **nothing is ever at risk.** The no-villains
rule (R2) means no episode has opposition — the patrol leave voluntarily, the
assessor helps, the magistrate wants the category to exist, and the finale's
antagonist is weather. Exactly one thing is ever in jeopardy across seven
episodes, and 007a resolves it with the tree comfortably able to pay.

Weakest episode: **004** — one gag, a delivery, a warning, the tree's
participation is four shots of a leaf *not* moving, and its hook is voided in
the first minute of 005.

## Entry point (this matters for distribution)

Vertical video is not watched in order, and the reader was explicit:

- **Stands alone:** 001 fully. 007a almost fully — only "fourteen days" and "a
  fig, once" need prior episodes.
- **Stands as a sketch:** 005.
- **Close to unwatchable cold:** 004 and 003b. Both are the second half of the
  episode before them. 004 opens *mid-sentence* on a ceremony from 003b, so a
  new viewer's first line is a callback to something they never heard, followed
  by "I had something prepared", which refers to nothing.

## Disposition

Nothing in this document was acted on the night it was written. The continuity
faults need dialogue changes, which need a re-voice; the three reserved findings
need the founder. Recorded so the next cycle starts from evidence instead of
from a fresh guess.
