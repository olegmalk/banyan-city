# Node 001 — ALTERNATE shot list for beats 07/08/09 (progression set)

**Not the live prompts.** `shots.md` is still the only file the pipeline reads; this
one exists so a proposal can be rendered and judged side by side with what is
already there, without editing the beats it is competing against.

Why it exists — the founder, 2026-08-07: *"also i noticed that beat 7, 8, 9 are
basically the same picture."* He is right, and the three prompts say so: 07 is "a
tiny two-leaf sprout... low close shot", 08 is "a single small leaf on a thin stem...
low close shot", 09 is "a tiny two-leaf green sprout... low close shot". Three beats,
one lens, one subject, one distance — thirteen seconds of the same frame.

The steward's earlier attempt to separate them changed the PALETTE (07 went cool and
overcast) and the founder caught that too: *"beat 7 makes everything look grayened…
it drastically changes the style."* So the separation has to come from somewhere that
is free — and the free axis is the LENS.

**The proposal: one scene, one light, three distances.** Wide establishing (07, the
field and how alone he is in it) → medium (08, the stillness arriving) → close (09,
`whoami`, the sprout as a face). Cutting in is a normal grammar for a beat that
narrows from "where am I" to "what am I", it is the shot progression the VO already
performs, and it costs nothing but three prompts. Palette and environment tokens are
deliberately IDENTICAL across the three — short green grass, damp brown soil, pale
blue morning sky, soft warm morning light, dead calm air — so the cut reads as three
shots of one place rather than three places.

**How the founder judges it:** four candidates per beat, rendered on the same seeds
as the singles in `shots.md`, so the only variable is the prompt. If he takes the
progression, these three blocks replace their counterparts in `shots.md`; if he takes
the singles, this file is deleted and the sameness gets solved another way. That is
his call (R4), not the steward's.

## Beat 07 — ZERO (0) MOVING PARTS (0:29–0:35) ⬜ needs footage07

WIDE. Establishing: the field, and one sprout alone in it. The stillness of the whole
place is the setup for the joke; the sprout is small in frame but close to camera, so
it is not the "small subject inside a vista" failure `style.md` warns about.

```
plant focus, no humans, wide establishing shot of an open green field at morning, one tiny two-leaf sprout alone in the lower centre of frame standing dead still, short green grass to a low horizon, pale blue morning sky, soft warm morning light, one thin cloud drifting, dead calm air, cinematic lighting, detailed, newest, masterpiece, best quality, very aesthetic No macro close-up, no many leaves, no grey sky, no overcast. No photorealism, no 3D render look. 9:16 vertical, no text.
```
