# Node 004 — shot list (20 shots, 1:1 with the script's beats)

Rebuilt 2026-07-25 for loop cycle 007 alongside `004-t0-b`. **One shot per
beat, 4–7s, camera on the referent of that beat's line** (SCRIPT-SPEC.md
"one beat = one shot"). The naming montage was one held shot across four
rejected names; each name and each refusal now has its own frame, so the
tree visibly participates in the scene it is the subject of.

Base footage only: no burned-in text, no dialogue — post adds captions, the
wireframe overlay flash, and VO. 9:16 vertical. Every prompt's FIRST
sentence carries the primary action (motion grammar, `style.md`).

**Character continuity (anime model sheet, `../../style.md`):** the
scavenger is a small round goblin — enormous expressive ears that act like
a second face, huge eyes, one broken tusk, patchwork cloak in faded greens
and browns. The farmer is a broad squarish silhouette in a straw hat, three
lines for his whole face, permanently unimpressed, carrying a clay jug. The
sapling is a tiny (~50cm) mascot-simple tree — thin curved trunk, two
oversized expressive leaves, one thin bare side-branch, never a face;
beside it a crooked lean-to and a small three-stone cairn.

**Assembly:** `python3 pipeline/render_t3.py sapling 004 --clips <dir> --out ep.mp4`
**Free render:** `python3 pipeline/kaggle/run_remote.py push 004`
