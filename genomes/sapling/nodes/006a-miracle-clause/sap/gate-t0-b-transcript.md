# Comprehension gate — 006a-t0-b (pre-release sap, brief §6.4)

**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Method:** fresh context-free reader, script text
only, one pass, five questions + per-beat camera check + speaker tracking.
**Verdict: PASS on story; the most detailed report of the cycle, and it
found a procedural error in a script about procedure.**

Reader: *"the bureaucratic engine is genuinely good and the fig beat is the
best thing in it."* All five story answers correct. Both hooks stated, and
the reader preferred the quieter one — *"to whom does the shrine get
dedicated… the ending would be stronger if the dedication were the last
thing said."* Praised as the best-executed thing in the script: the VO
identifying itself in its first sentence.

Fixed before any render:

1. **The document was sealed and handed over BEFORE the consent that
   authorises it.** *"For a script whose entire joke is procedural
   correctness, that's the one continuity error the audience will actually
   catch."* Re-ordered: consent → recorded → stamp → tear free → hand over.
2. **The form materialised from nowhere** two thirds in and then ran the
   rest of the episode. New beat **THE FORM**: she draws it from her robes
   when the ruling lands.
3. **The tree was two sizes again** — man-height in the cold open, yet she
   looks "up into the crown" three times and the goblin hides behind its
   trunk. Now her face is level with the crown (never "up into"), and he
   dives behind the lean-to.
4. **Nobody ever says who she is** — her authority came from a script label
   the audience can't see, in a beat that had no dialogue at all. The VO now
   introduces her: *"The assessor writes things down. She decides what they
   are."*
5. **"Community service" had no on-screen setup** — now attributed out loud
   to the assessor.
6. **Apple vs fig** pointed at two different trees; the goblin now says
   *"He grew it."*
7. **A line answering the wrong question** — "he's more of a roommate" now
   explicitly answers the livestock question.
8. **Unfilmable acting notes** ("her face does almost nothing; something
   behind it recalculates") replaced with actions (she takes the quill from
   behind her ear, looks at it, puts it back).
9. **Over-packed beats trimmed** (the clause, the career-trajectory joke,
   the audit-prep line) and the moon deadline made concrete: *"Fourteen
   nights."*
10. **Duplicate ledger close-ups** differentiated; the ending re-ordered so
    the dedication is the last spoken line and the final image is the blank
    ruled line.

**Open and NOT fixed — for the author (R4):** *"I finished the script not
knowing what to call him."* The protagonist has no name anywhere in the
series; "Shade" is the settlement. Naming him — or deciding he stays
unnamed — is a taste call, not a steward call.
