## State change (R1)

The magistrate reads the ledger line — **"Occupation: answers questions"** — and
the law finds its category: the tree is registered a **shrine**, the settlement's
fees are waived, and the scavenger is appointed its **keeper**. A fugitive whose
entire legal existence was one apple now holds *papers*. And the tree consents
to all of it, on the record, with one leaf.

## Hook (R5)

Registration is provisional on one formality: a shrine must demonstrate
**one (1) verified wonder, witnessed by an officer of rank**. The magistrate
returns at the full moon. Two weeks to arrange a miracle, no definition
provided, or the category collapses back into "taxable orchard" — taking the
keeper's papers down with it.

## Script

**COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:06**
A tree the height of a man, small crown, side branches; below it a goblin re-stacks three cairn stones by size, steps back, and re-stacks them by colour.

> **VO (dry, tired, engineer):** I died an engineer and woke up as this tree. This morning my town is being *staged*.

**AUDIT PREP — 0:06–0:10**
The farmer walks in and sets a second clay jug down beside the first, squaring it up.

> **FARMER:** Infrastructure.

**YOU CANNOT MOP — 0:10–0:15**
The goblin drags a branch across the dirt, sweeping the clearing smooth.

> **VO:** I've seen this before. It's called audit prep. Everyone tries.

**SHE IS EARLY — 0:15–0:20**
The MAGISTRATE is already inside the settlement — travel-worn robes of office, a seal of office on a chain, reading the assessor's ledger as she walks.

> **VO:** That is a magistrate. The assessor writes things down. She decides what they *are*.

**REFLEX — 0:20–0:25**
The goblin dives behind the lean-to out of pure reflex — then remembers the assessor gave him a job, and emerges to stand at approximate attention.

**READING THE FILE — 0:25–0:30**
She reads without looking up; the page turns under her thumb.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Settlement, Shade. Founded yesterday, structures one-*ish*, population three.

**RESIDENT NUMBER THREE — 0:30–0:35**
She stops walking and reads the last line twice, her thumb marking the place.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Occupation of resident number three: *"answers questions."*

**WELL? — 0:35–0:38**
She closes the ledger with one hand and looks straight into the crown, level with her own face.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Well?

**ONE LEAF — 0:38–0:43**
In still air, one leaf tilts — and holds.

**THE TIDY EXPLANATION — 0:43–0:49**
She takes the quill from behind her ear, looks at it, and puts it back — the only sign of a mind changing.

> **MAGISTRATE:** …So the assessor hasn't been drinking. Pity. That was the tidy explanation.

**NOT A RESIDENT — 0:49–0:54**
She begins to pace, counting categories off on her fingers.

> **MAGISTRATE:** It cannot be a *resident* — residents owe hearth tax, and it has no hearth.

**NOT PROPERTY — 0:54–1:01**
She turns on her heel, one finger raised.

> **MAGISTRATE:** It cannot be *property* — whoever claims it inherits liability for one documented apple incident.

**NO TAKERS — 1:01–1:05**
The goblin raises a hand halfway, then puts it down.

> **SCAVENGER:** No takers.
> **MAGISTRATE:** Shocking.

**NOT LIVESTOCK — 1:05–1:10**
She looks at the tree's trunk, then at the ground it stands in.

> **MAGISTRATE:** It cannot be *livestock* — livestock can be moved. Can it be moved?

**A ROOMMATE — 1:10–1:15**
The goblin gestures between himself and the tree, improvising.

> **SCAVENGER:** No — no, he's not livestock, he's more of a *roommate*—

**DO YOU HAVE A DEED — 1:15–1:20**
She stops and holds out one empty hand for a document.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Roommates are a tenancy. Tenancies have deeds. Do you have a deed?

**I HAVE A FIG — 1:20–1:24**
He searches his patchwork cloak, then produces a single fig and offers it.

> **SCAVENGER:** …I have a fig. He grew it.

**THE ANSWER ARRIVES — 1:24–1:31**
She stops pacing and looks slowly at the jugs at the roots, the cairn, the two of them — a life arranged around a tree in one day.

> **MAGISTRATE:** The kingdom has exactly one category for a thing that stands in one place, belongs to no one, and answers questions.

**SHRINE — 1:31–1:34**
She says the word straight into the crown.

> **MAGISTRATE:** *Shrine.*

**A SCRIBBLE — 1:34–1:40**
Close on the leaves, motionless, absorbing it.

> **VO:** Engineer. Tree. Town. Now a *church*. My career has stopped being a line.

**THE FORM — 1:40–1:45**
She draws a folded form from inside her robes, opens it flat against her forearm, and starts filling it as she recites; behind her the farmer's crossed arms come loose.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Shrine grounds are fee-exempt. Shrines require registered upkeep. Therefore—

**DO YOU CONSENT — 1:45–1:51**
She turns to the tree, pen ready over the consent field.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Registration requires consent of the venerated object. Do you consent?

**CONSENT RECORDED — 1:51–1:58**
One leaf tilts against the sky. Her pen moves once.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Consent recorded. First signature I've taken all year that didn't argue.

**THE STAMP — 1:58–2:03**
She presses a wax stamp onto the page and lifts it away clean.

**CONGRATULATIONS — 2:03–2:10**
She tears the slip free and holds it out; the goblin takes it in both hands.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Shrines have keepers. Congratulations. The assessor's community service is reclassified: *employment.*

**A LINE ITEM — 2:10–2:15**
He turns the paper over once, finds the seal on the back, and stops.

> **SCAVENGER (quietly, wrecked):** I've never been a line item before.

**THE CLAUSE — 2:15–2:24**
She stops on the form's final line and, for the first time, looks faintly entertained.

> **MAGISTRATE:** One formality. Provisional until the shrine demonstrates a "verified wonder, witnessed by an officer of rank." Fourteen nights.

**SURPRISE ME — 2:24–2:30**
The goblin hurries two steps after her, waving the paper; she keeps walking.

> **SCAVENGER:** What— what qualifies? Is there a list? A form?
> **MAGISTRATE:** Surprise me.

**A NORMAL RELEASE — 2:30–2:36**
She walks away up the road; the goblin stares at his paper and the farmer looks up at the tree.

> **VO:** Fourteen days to ship a miracle. No spec. One reviewer. …So — a normal release.

**THE DEDICATION LINE — 2:36–2:43**
At the clearing's edge she stops, turns, and speaks back across the distance.

> **MAGISTRATE:** One thing. The dedication line I left blank — shrines declare *to whom*. Those words outlive towns.

**BLANK — 2:43–2:48**
Close on the paper in the goblin's hands: one ruled line, empty.

SMASH TO BLACK.

```
SHADE · shrine (provisional) · MIRACLE: due full moon
```

## Taste-rule notes

- **R1:** three irreversible changes — the tree gains a legal category, the
  settlement gains fee-exemption, the scavenger gains papers. Each has a cost:
  the category carries a deadline, the exemption depends on it, the papers
  dissolve if it collapses.
- **R2:** the magistrate is jurisprudence, not menace — she *wants* the
  category to exist, and the threat is a clause, not a person.
- **R3:** the legal crisis resolves by absurdity-escalation: the law defeats
  resident, property, livestock, and tenancy in turn until only *shrine* — the
  most ridiculous and most correct answer — is left standing. Both sides end
  up better off, laughing distance from where they started.
- **R5:** the hook is a real state change armed with a timer: a bureaucratically
  mandated miracle, undefined, due at the full moon, with the keeper's papers
  as the stake.
