# 007a comprehension gate — two independent cold readers (2026-07-25)

Both readers got only `node.md` and `shots.md`, were told they had never seen
episodes 1-6, and were forbidden every other file in the repo. They did not see
each other's work. Scores: **6/10** and **5/10**.

They agree, unprompted, on almost everything — which is why this is worth
keeping. The agreements are findings; the disagreements are noted inline.

## What both readers said works

- The premise lands in about six seconds ("I died an engineer and woke up as
  this tree"). Both got the state change unaided.
- Both phrased the closing question unprompted, which is the R7 test:
  *what does the kneeling stranger want, and can the tree give it?*
- Both found the comedy stretch (beats 04-16) nearly clean, and both named
  "Nobody booked the *wind*" over the horizon-wide as the best line/image pair.
- **Both explicitly rejected "the video doesn't match the audio at all" as a
  description of the shot list as it now stands.** Tallies were 21 MATCH /
  6 DRIFT / 2 CONTRADICTS and 19 MATCH / 8 DRIFT / 2 CONTRADICTS.

## What both readers said is broken

The drift is not spread out. It is concentrated in beats 17-23 — the emotional
climax — where the camera sits on a gesture instead of on the thing the line is
about. One reader: *"that's exactly where I stopped following."*

1. **The cost is never spoken, only glowed.** The tree spending two weeks of
   stored growth is the most important fact in the episode and exists in the
   `## State change` notes, not in any line. Beat 20 is seven seconds of teal
   light in roots; with no established grammar, both read it as generic magic,
   and read beat 22's wilt as *tired* rather than *spent*.
2. **Beat 19's "a fig, once" is the emotional hinge and a stranger has no fig.**
   The chest-press gesture is opaque; the event is in episode 2.
3. **The deadline has no setter and no penalty.** Fourteen days imposed by
   nobody, failure costs nothing. Both flagged ~2 minutes of rehearsal comedy
   against an unmeasurable clock.
4. **Beat 10: the moon rises at 0:56 after nine explicitly moonlit beats** —
   and it follows a line about *sunrise*, so the rising light reads as dawn,
   then it is night for two more minutes. Both called it a contradiction.
5. **Beat 26: the figs teleport.** On the branches at 2:31, on the ground at
   2:45, with no fall shown, and the tree is suddenly bare-leafed when beat 22
   showed leaves wilting *on* it.
6. **Beat 29 introduces two institutions in the closing line.** "Patrol wanted
   taxes. Assessor wanted a count." — the most series-dependent moment is the
   last thing the viewer hears. One reader also flagged it as the densest line
   in the script (29 words) over the slowest hold.
7. **Beat 28 puts two speakers in a shot containing neither.** "First *what?*"
   comes from a goblin who is not in frame, about a pilgrim who is not in frame,
   answered by a farmer walking away.
8. **"SCAVENGER" is a caption label nobody ever says aloud.** Eight of nineteen
   lines carry it; both readers scanned the frame for a scavenger and found a
   goblin.
9. **"Shade" and "shrine" appear only on the end card**, never in dialogue —
   and beat 18's "Shade. Water. Answers." collides with SHADE-the-place, which
   was never established. One reader read it as literal shade.
10. **Beat 17's "He doesn't *do* wonders"** is a close-up on the goblin with the
    tree out of frame; "he" has no on-screen antecedent and the previous speaker
    was the farmer.
11. **Beat 06's "branch three" is uncountable** — nothing distinguishes which
    branch is third.
12. **The header contradicted the script**: `~150s · 26 beats` against a 193s,
    29-beat script. Both caught it. Fixed.

Where they disagreed: beat 03 (one MATCH, one DRIFT — the VO plays over the
goblin's close-up just before he first speaks, which the second reader argued
teaches the wrong narrator); and beat 23, where one flagged impossible geometry
(a tall magistrate walking *under* a 1.6 m tree, per the growth ladder) and the
other flagged that a *raised* palm reads as taking when the beat's point is that
the tree gives. Both objections are right and have the same fix.

## Disposition

Fixed the same night, in `shots.md` and the header — no dialogue touched, so the
voice track stays valid: the moonrise contradiction (beats 01-10 re-lit), the
fig fall (26), the climax framing (03, 17, 19, 28), countable branches (05-06),
beat 23's geometry and the unbidden fig, and the header.

**Deferred, because they need dialogue and dialogue changes need a re-voice:**
faults 1, 3, 6, 8, 9 — naming the cost, naming who set the deadline, recasting
the closing line as in-episode fact, saying the keeper's name aloud, and putting
"Shade"/"shrine" into a line. Proposals are recorded in
`pipeline/loop/cycle-008-fix-list.md`; several of them collide with series-level
decisions reserved to the author (R4), including whether the protagonist gets a
name at all.
