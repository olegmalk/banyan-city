# Comprehension gate — 005-t0-b (pre-release sap, brief §6.4)

**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Method:** fresh context-free reader, script text
only, one pass, five questions + per-beat camera check + an added question
about speaker tracking (this episode has three speakers plus the narrator).
**Verdict: PASS on story; eight faults found and fixed, one of them a
self-contradiction inside the same episode.**

Story answers correct; the reader called the ending *"the strongest part of
the script"* and stated both hooks — the magistrate, and the unseen line on
the occupation page. Twelve beats passed with no notes.

Faults, worst first, all fixed:

1. **A beat that contradicted its own episode.** "HE'S BEEN WATCHING" cut to
   three earlier moments of leaves moving — moments that were never filmed,
   and one earlier tree close-up explicitly said the leaves were
   *motionless*. It was also the only beat that could not be one take.
   Fixed both ways: two real leaf-twitches are now planted on answer beats
   (FOUNDED, THREE STONES), and the realisation is one unbroken shot of the
   assessor simply looking at the tree.
2. **Speaker tracking fails on screen even though the script is labelled.**
   *"Yes in the script, no on screen."* The goblin answered from off-frame
   before we had ever seen him; the farmer materialised at 1:15 having only
   been mentioned in VO. Worst case: at WATER SOURCE the reader would have
   assigned "the channel mended itself" to the goblin, *"which wrecks the
   beat."* Fixed: the goblin steps into frame to answer, the farmer gets his
   own establishing beat, and both men share the frame when he stonewalls.
3. **Three consecutive "assessor measures a thing" shots**, two of them the
   same knotted string on a different noun. The third is now him crouching
   and writing without measuring at all — *"vary the verb, not the object."*
4. **Scale/posture unstated** — he "measures upward into the crown" and
   "addresses" a knee-high tree. Now he crouches, then kneels, said out loud
   in both beats and in the prompts.
5. **The apple contradiction** — "Zero fruit. Historically" / "One apple" /
   "it fell off a cart" left the reader unsure whether the tree has ever
   fruited. The assessor now says *"Not the tree's"* explicitly.
6. **A miscount in a film about accurate counting** — the quill "writes two
   words" for a three-word sentence. Fixed.
7. **ONE LEAF framing ambiguous** ("three held faces" reads as three
   singles plus an insert). Now specified as one unbroken wide.
8. **Two beats where the shot didn't earn its line** (the road walk, the
   counting) — noted, deliberately kept: the counting beat plants the payoff.

Carried to the voice mix, not the script: the reader warned that the tree's
VO and the assessor are both written "dry", and *"on the page they're
distinct because one is labelled; in the ear they may not be."* The measured
pitch bands (`qa_voices.py`) put the tree at ~108 Hz and the assessor at
~132 Hz, and captions now carry speaker names — but this is worth listening
for specifically when 005 is re-voiced.
