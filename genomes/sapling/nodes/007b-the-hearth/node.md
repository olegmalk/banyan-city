## State change (R1)

The census bug is found and fixed: the sky counts **hearths** — a person is
where their fire is — so the scavenger builds the flammable resident a fire
pit, the tree confirms its own dialog *instantly* (it has learned what
dithering costs), and sky and ledger reconcile: **pop. 3**, and the `(?)`
that has hung over Shade since 004 resolves. The system is now *certain*
about them for the first time. And certainty, it turns out, is **logged**.

## Hook (R5)

The final line of the sunset window: `Δ pop.: +1 · logged`. A log implies a
log *reader*. Being uncounted was dangerous — but being counted means Shade
now appears in the feed of whatever corrected that unremembered town. They
fixed the discrepancy; they created a record. Something tails this log.

## Script

**COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:06**
Sunset, inside the wireframe window. The SCAVENGER sits bolt upright with a charcoal stick and a bark slab, taking dictation from a tree.

> **VO (dry, tired, engineer):** I am a dead engineer inside a tree, and the sky refuses to count me.

**DICTATION — 0:06–0:11**
The leaves tilt — once, twice — and the charcoal scratches after them, too slow.

> **VO:** To fix a count, you find out what the counter counts.

**FOUR CHARACTERS — 0:11–0:16**
The lattice drains out of the world. Close on the slab: four charcoal characters, and a lot of empty bark.

**THREE SECONDS A DAY — 0:16–0:20**
He adds the slab to a leaning stack of slabs by the roots.

> **VO:** Data collection rate: three seconds per day.

**SLOWER DASHBOARDS — 0:20–0:25**
The scavenger's face over the stack, counting them with a claw.

> **VO:** I have had slower dashboards. They were at least *paid* for.

**DAY ONE — 0:25–0:29**
Wireframe: the farmhouse across the field, smoke at the chimney, its label lit.

```
dwelling · hearth: 1
```

> **VO:** Day one. The farmhouse: dwelling, hearth one.

**DAY TWO — 0:29–0:33**
Wireframe: the lean-to, sagging, its label smaller and unsure.

```
structure(?)
```

> **VO:** Day two. Our lean-to: structure, question mark.

**DAY THREE — 0:33–0:39**
Wireframe: the three-stone cairn, wearing a label of its own.

```
monument(?)
```

> **VO:** Day three. The cairn: monument, question mark. The sky is unsure about *rocks*.

**DAY FOUR — 0:39–0:44**
The FARMER stands motionless in his field inside the wireframe, and a count tick renders beside him.

> **VO:** Day four. The farmer stands still, and is counted.

**AND ME — 0:44–0:48**
The tree stands just as still. Nothing renders beside it at all.

> **VO:** I stand still. I am not.

**THE CASE BOARD — 0:48–0:54**
The scavenger turns the big slab around: four findings scrawled down it like a case board.

> **VO:** There it is. The sky doesn't count bodies. It counts *fires*.

**A PERSON IS A HEARTH — 0:54–0:58**
The farmhouse chimney, smoke climbing steadily into a darkening sky.

> **VO:** A person is where their hearth is.

**THE HEARTH TAX — 0:58–1:03**
A kingdom notice nailed to a roadside post, its column headed *hearths*, tallies inked beneath.

> **VO:** And the kingdom taxes by hearth. Their law isn't law.

**A CACHED COPY — 1:03–1:09**
Wireframe wide: the whole valley in lattice, every distant farmhouse carrying the same label.

> **VO:** It's a *cached copy of the schema*. Mirrored for centuries and called tradition.

**THE ASSIGNMENT — 1:09–1:13**
The scavenger reads the slab the tree has just dictated. His ears go all the way down.

> **SCAVENGER:** You want a *fire*. Next to *you*.

**KINDLING WITH OPINIONS — 1:13–1:18**
Close on his appalled face, one hand raised in apology for what he is about to say.

> **SCAVENGER:** You are — I say this with respect — *kindling with opinions*.

**THE STONES — 1:18–1:23**
He sets ring stones one by one into the soil between the roots, each placed with visible terror.

**THE FIRE BRIGADE — 1:23–1:27**
The farmer takes up position beside the pit with a full jug, doing grim arithmetic.

> **FARMER:** One spark and your whole religion is charcoal.

**ROOMMATE — 1:27–1:31**
The scavenger, arms out, defending the arrangement.

> **SCAVENGER:** He's not a religion. He's my roommate.

**WORSE — 1:31–1:35**
The farmer, unimpressed, jug unmoved.

> **FARMER:** Worse.

**THE FLAME CATCHES — 1:35–1:40**
A tiny flame catches inside the stone ring and steadies.

**THE LEAVES PULL BACK — 1:40–1:44**
Every leaf on the tree draws visibly back from the light.

> **VO:** There is a fire inside my root perimeter.

**WORST UPTIME STRATEGY — 1:44–1:51**
Close on the flame from the trunk's side, bark warm-lit and much too close.

> **VO:** I am made of wood. This is the worst uptime strategy I have ever personally approved.

**SUNSET — 1:51–1:55**
The sun touches the horizon and the lattice blooms out across the field.

**THE LEAN-TO RE-RENDERS — 1:55–2:00**
The lean-to's label rewrites itself first, and beyond it the settlement label starts to flicker.

```
dwelling · hearth: 1
```

**THE DIALOG — 2:00–2:05**
Over the trunk a dialog opens — the first the tree has ever been shown.

```
UNRESOLVED ENTITY · hearth detected
VERIFY RESIDENT: Y/N
```

**SEVENTEEN HOURS — 2:05–2:10**
Close on the dialog with the leaves hanging beside it.

> **VO:** Last time the sky asked this, someone took seventeen hours.

**THE HOURS BACK — 2:10–2:14**
The leaves, already gathering themselves.

> **VO:** You never get the hours back.

**Y — 2:14–2:18**
One leaf swings up and strikes **Y**, instantly.

**THE CASCADE — 2:18–2:23**
The labels cascade, fast, one overwriting the next.

```
RESIDENT: verified
pop. 2 → 3
```

**NO QUESTION MARK — 2:23–2:28**
Over the clearing, the settlement label rewrites itself.

```
SHADE · settlement · pop. 3
```

> **VO:** No question mark. The sky is *sure* about us.

**THE LAST LINE — 2:28–2:33**
The window starts to die, and one more line renders small in the corner, system-toned, meant for nobody.

```
Δ pop.: +1 · logged
```

**LOGGED — 2:33–2:37**
The world snaps solid. Dusk, crickets, the little fire crackling between the roots.

> **VO (quiet):** Logged.

**THE CHEER — 2:37–2:42**
The scavenger cheers at a sky he cannot read while the farmer pours a precautionary arc of water around the pit.

**THE OTHER SIDE — 2:42–2:47**
Long hold on the small ringed fire.

> **VO:** I spent a whole career on the other side of that word.

**THE HOOK — 2:47–2:53**
The fire, and the dark standing over it.

> **VO:** Logs don't exist for the people *in* them. Something tails this log.

SMASH TO BLACK.

```
SHADE · pop. 3 (reconciled) · CHANGE: logged
```

## Taste-rule notes

- **Cycle 007 (shot density), applied 2026-08-10:** 36 beats / ~173s = a cut
  every 4.8s at 0.75 spoken lines per shot, against the predecessor's 5 shots
  for the same material. The investigation was the worst offender: four days
  of findings lived in one code block under one held image, and each day is
  now its own wireframe picture with its own line. Nothing was added — the
  speeches were split at the sentence, so no beat carries more than ~6
  seconds of voice and no clip has to loop to cover its line.
- **R1:** the tree gains a hearth (a legal and systemic *address*), the count
  reconciles, the settlement's `(?)` resolves, and the change is logged —
  each irreversible, the last one ominously so.
- **R2:** no villain — the danger is an audit trail, and everyone on screen
  is trying to keep everyone else alive (the farmer's jug, the ringed
  stones, the leaves pulling back).
- **R3:** the crisis of being uncountable resolves through honest absurdity:
  personhood achieved via *fire code*. "Kindling with opinions" carries the
  episode's fear in a joke, and now has the close-up to land on.
- **R5:** the hook is one rendered line that recontextualizes the victory:
  they didn't just fix a count, they created a record — and records have
  readers.
- **R7:** the cold open re-grounds the premise (dead engineer, inside a tree,
  uncounted) in one shot and one line.
