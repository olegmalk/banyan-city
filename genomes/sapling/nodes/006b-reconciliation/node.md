**Tree:** Sapling · **Parent:** [005 — The Assessor](../005-the-assessor/node.md) · **Trunk:** undecided · **Status:** hot

## State change (R1)

The kingdom's bureaucracy is revealed for what it is: **a monitoring system**.
Settlement reviews happen at last light because the review *is* the overlay —
the magistrate reads the sunset wireframe the way the tree does, and her
office exists to reconcile the kingdom's ledgers with what the sky renders.
And the two now disagree about Shade: the ledger says **population three**;
the sky still says **two**. The tree is officially recorded — and officially
uncounted — at the same time.

## Hook (R5)

Discrepancies get *corrected* — and the last time the sky refused to count
what a ledger held, it stopped counting an entire town, which now exists only
on archive maps the magistrate is not permitted to remember. She would rather
amend the sky than the ledger; no one has ever been permitted to try. Shade
has until her next circuit to be countable.

## Script

**COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:06**
Dusk over the clearing: the young tree, the crooked lean-to, the three-stone cairn, the clay jug — and one dark figure standing at the road's edge.

> **VO (dry, tired, engineer):** I am a dead engineer living inside a tree. The kingdom just sent someone.

**REGULATION ONE — 0:06–0:11**
The MAGISTRATE, alone at the clearing's edge: dark robes of office, a seal on a chain, an open pocket-watch in her hand. Nobody saw her come up the road.

> **MAGISTRATE (not looking up from the watch):** Settlement review is conducted at last light. Regulation One.

**FROZEN MID-BITE — 0:11–0:15**
The SCAVENGER, bread halfway to his mouth, stops moving. His ears go down.

> **VO:** Not rule one of many. Rule *One*.

**ALWAYS AN OUTAGE — 0:15–0:21**
Close on the pocket-watch turning in her fingers, its lid open, the dusk going gold in the metal.

> **VO:** Every org has a rule nobody remembers the reason for. It's always an outage.

**THE HEADCOUNT — 0:21–0:26**
The FARMER and the scavenger shuffle themselves into a nervous, evenly spaced row.

> **VO:** They have arranged themselves into a headcount. Nobody asked them to.

**THE LEDGER — 0:26–0:31**
Her hands open the assessor's ledger; last light lies flat across the page.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Shade. Founded yesterday. Structures: one-*ish*.

**ONE-ISH — 0:31–0:36**
Cut to the lean-to: deadfall against a rock, sagging visibly to one side.

> **MAGISTRATE (off):** I will be speaking to the assessor about "one-ish".

**MENDED ITSELF — 0:36–0:40**
The clay jug beside the mended channel, clear water running past it.

> **MAGISTRATE (off):** Water: "mended itself". Him too.

**POPULATION THREE — 0:40–0:46**
Her finger travels down the page and stops on a line.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Population: three. Occupation of resident number three — *(beat)* "answers questions".

**THE SNAP — 0:46–0:52**
She closes the ledger, checks the watch, snaps it shut in one movement.

> **MAGISTRATE:** The assessor is thorough. So either he is wrong, or the sky is.

**THE SKY? — 0:52–0:56**
The farmer and the scavenger turn their heads and look at each other.

> **SCAVENGER:** …The sky, ma'am?
> **MAGISTRATE:** Four minutes.

**RECONCILE — 0:56–1:02**
Camera on the tree, leaves hanging very still in the last of the light.

> **VO:** She is not here to judge us. She is here to *reconcile* us.

**LEDGER VERSUS PRODUCTION — 1:02–1:07**
The ledger tucked under her arm, seal swinging against it.

> **VO:** Ledger versus production. I used to be this woman.

**A WORSE ROBE — 1:07–1:12**
Her silhouette against the low sun, robes moving in the field wind.

> **VO:** At three in the morning. In a worse robe.

**LAST LIGHT — 1:12–1:16**
The sun touches the horizon; the whole field goes long and gold and the shadows run out sideways.

**THE WINDOW — 1:16–1:20**
The world flickers to **wireframe** — grass a lattice, the cairn low-poly, the jug a labeled cylinder.

**STILL TALKING — 1:20–1:25**
The scavenger, oblivious inside the wireframe, gesturing at the jug with both hands.

> **SCAVENGER:** The water's very reliable now, ma'am, whatever the form says—

**MA'AM? — 1:25–1:29**
His hands come down. His ears swivel toward her back.

> **SCAVENGER:** …Is she having a moment? Ma'am?

**READING THE SKY — 1:29–1:33**
Low angle on the magistrate: she has turned her back on all of them, chin up, eyes tracking something in the air above the clearing.

**THE LABEL — 1:33–1:37**
Her eyeline: over the clearing, the label renders.

```
SHADE · settlement(?) · pop. 2
```

> **VO:** It says two. The ledger says three.

**THE SEAL — 1:37–1:42**
She lifts the seal on its chain into the light; for one frame it renders in wireframe with its own tag: `seal_of_office · auth: valid`.

> **VO:** Her seal renders too. Valid. She is *in* the system.

**THE REVEAL — 1:42–1:48**
Close on the tree's leaves with the wireframe world behind them.

> **VO (dawning):** She can see the debug layer. It was never bureaucracy. It's *monitoring*.

**CENTURIES — 1:48–1:55**
Wireframe wide: the whole valley in lattice, distant farmhouses each carrying a small label.

> **VO:** They've read the console for centuries and built a civilization around never admitting it.

**WHEN THEY DISAGREE — 1:55–2:01**
She steps close to the trunk, humans far behind her and out of earshot; her face beside the leaves, eyes still up.

> **MAGISTRATE (quietly):** The ledger says three. The sky says two. One of them gets corrected.

**THE WINDOW CLOSES — 2:01–2:05**
The lattice collapses out of the world. Solid dusk, warm air, crickets.

**ROUTINE AGAIN — 2:05–2:10**
She turns back to the humans, brisk, entirely an official again.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Findings noted. Review pending. Next circuit at harvest's end.

**AT THE ROAD — 2:10–2:16**
Her back, stopped at the road exactly where the assessor paused, the dark ahead of her.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Last time a ledger held a name the sky would not count—

**THE VALLEY — 2:16–2:21**
A wide dark valley at night with no lights anywhere in it.

> **MAGISTRATE (off):** —the sky stopped counting the whole valley.

**THE ARCHIVE MAP — 2:21–2:26**
An old drawn map under glass, a town inked on it, the ink gone brown.

> **MAGISTRATE (off):** There are maps in the archive with a town on them.

**NOT PERMITTED — 2:26–2:31**
Close on her face, half-turned, giving nothing away.

> **MAGISTRATE:** A town I am not permitted to remember.

**BE COUNTABLE — 2:31–2:36**
She turns her head far enough to put the tree in her eyeline.

> **MAGISTRATE:** Be countable by harvest's end.

**AMEND THE SKY — 2:36–2:41**
Her face, one eyebrow, the last colour draining out of the sky behind her.

> **MAGISTRATE:** I would rather amend the sky than the ledger.

**NO ONE HAS TRIED — 2:41–2:46**
She looks down at the seal in her hand.

> **MAGISTRATE (quieter):** No one has ever been permitted to try.

**INTO THE DARK — 2:46–2:50**
She walks away down the road and the dark takes her, robes last.

**TWO WEEKS — 2:50–2:55**
Long hold on the tree, leaves very still against the dying light.

> **VO:** Two weeks to convince the sky that I exist.

**THE HOOK — 2:55–3:02**
Close on one leaf as the light goes off it.

> **VO:** I've filed tickets against systems that didn't believe in me. I usually lost.

SMASH TO BLACK.


  006a is the R3-strong one. The pair exists so the choice is felt on
  material (R4), not argued.

  harvest's end — armed with the heaviest precedent the story has shown.
