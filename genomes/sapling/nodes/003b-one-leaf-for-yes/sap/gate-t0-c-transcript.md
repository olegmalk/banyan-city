**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Method:** fresh context-free reader, script text
only, one pass, five questions + the cycle-007 per-beat camera check.
**Verdict: PASS on story (all five answered correctly), FAIL-then-FIXED on
ten craft flags.** The reader was asked to be blunt and was.

Story answers all correct, including the cost mechanic: *"there's a limited
daily energy budget, shown as root glow underground… it's the clearest
piece of worldbuilding in the script."* Hook landed: *"The smash-to-black
on the breath is the right cut and I felt it."* Favourite line, unprompted:
*"'I have onboarded senior engineers with less' told me who the narrator
used to be faster than any of the exposition did."*

Ten flags, all fixed before any render:

1. **Tree geometry broke (worst).** A "two-leaf sapling" cannot have
   dropped a fig; beats variously implied a sapling and a mature fig tree.
   *"Pick one and make every beat describe the same object."* Now defined
   once in both files: two oversized leaves + ONE thin bare side-branch
   where the fig grew, repeated in every prompt.
2. **Cold-open attribution** — "two green hands" read as the tree's own.
   Now explicitly the goblin's.
3. **NO WIND served the wrong sentence** (picture: checking still grass;
   line: proposing the protocol). Split into two beats — NO WIND, then a
   new **THE PROTOCOL** with him leaning in to make the treaty.
4. **THE BUDGET** — line was about the two of them, camera was on roots.
   Now a ground-level two-shot; the root-glow visual moved to TOWARD.
5. **TWO LEAVES** claimed a cost the frame couldn't show. The root glow now
   visibly dims and dies in the same frame as the tilt.
6. **"Filing problem"** presumed unseen bureaucracy. Replaced with
   *"The patrol has a form for apples. There isn't one for me."*
7. **"water down the way"** listed an asset never shown; the line now lists
   only what the viewer has seen.
8. **Five identical tilt framings.** Each is now specified distinctly
   (side-on macro / low against sky / stem-joint macro / two-shot with his
   face / wide including the root line).
9. **Two "man arranges himself" beats back to back.** THE TEST is now an
   over-the-shoulder POV.
10. **Two "hands in the dirt" beats at the emotional turn** — THE FILING
    PROBLEM now sits back against the trunk, gesturing.

Left deliberately: the god question's half-tilt, which the reader read as
*"the writer stalling rather than the character stalling."* It is the
designed 002c graft (the ADMIN(?) mystery) and stays a taste call for the
author (R4).
