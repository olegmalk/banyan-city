# motion-poscontrol-plates-0816 -- test fixtures, NOT show content

Two third-party still photographs, published here so the box's plate guard can
fetch and measure them like any other farm-out plate. They exist ONLY to answer
"can LTX-2.3 distilled move anything at all from a still?" under conditions
chosen to make the engine succeed: photoreal (in-distribution for a video
model), one clear subject, uncluttered background, and an action that is the
single most common motion in any video corpus.

They are NOT canon, NOT a style reference, and must never be imitated or
published as Sapling footage. Bytes are the unmodified originals from Wikimedia
Commons; the sha256 below is therefore the sha256 of the public file and is
independently verifiable by anyone.
