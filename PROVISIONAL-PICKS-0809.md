# The wave-level diagnosis — four defects, not twenty accidents

This is the part worth acting on. Episode 2's r3 wave was drawn to answer
*"competent frames of twenty different shows"*. It did not, and the reasons are
mechanical and fixable in the wave script.

**1 — The recurring character is missing.** Beats 02, 03, 13, 15, 17 and 19 all
name the sapling and none of the twenty-four frames contains one. Where the
checkpoint does draw it, it draws it as a **mascot with a face**: a round blob
creature (13, all four), a sprout growing out of the goblin's head (19, all
four), a leaf with eyes (16-s0), a fruit with eyes (18-s1, 12-s0). Beat 01
settled this in round 3 with `no chibi, no mascot, no creature, no face` written
comma-terminated so `sd_prompt` lifts them into the negative — **those terms are
evidently not on beats 02-21.** Port them across the wave. This is A1, weight 5,
his dominant objection, and it is one line of prompt plumbing.

**2 — `no humans` is not firing.** Human figures and human hands appear in b01
r5 (3 of 4), b14 (3 of 4), b20 (2 of 4), b21 (2 of 4) and b15-s1 — in beats whose
own text says the field is empty. The word `humans` alone is not binding this
checkpoint; the terms that bind it are `1girl, 1boy, child, person, hand`.

**3 — The guards are the wrong genre, and half of them are at night.** Beats
05–11 ask for *"round harmless shapes in mismatched ill-fitting armor"* in a
**morning field**. What came back is grim dark-fantasy: glowing visors, black
hoods, a drawn blade, black backgrounds. The comedy inverts into menace and the
environment inverts into night. `round, harmless, comedic, chibi-proportioned,
daylight` need to lead the guard clause, with `dark fantasy, night, glowing eyes,
hood, weapon, sword, knife, armor plate` negated.

**4 — The register swings inside one episode.** Beats 02/03/13 are flat cartoon
mascots; 05–11 are moody dark-fantasy; 16/18/21 are the soft cinematic anime the
show actually is. That is the *"twenty different shows"* complaint, still
standing, and it is why only the three beats that contain nothing but a plant
survived this pass — those are the only ones where no character had to be drawn.

**The ONE SAMPLE rule applies to all four fixes together** (founder,
2026-08-03). Fix the negatives, render **one** frame of beat 13 — the beat where
the sapling failed worst — and look at it before any set of twenty. The K recipe
cost an hour by skipping exactly this step.
