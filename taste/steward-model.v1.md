# Steward model — a predictor of the founder's taste — v1

**This is not a taste file. It is a falsifiable model OF one.**
`taste/sapling.founder.v0.3.md` is the founder's own file and the only place his
rules live; this file is the steward's attempt to *predict* what he will say
before he says it, so machines can render ahead of him instead of idling for
him. Every axis below is derived from words he actually typed, quoted verbatim
with its date and the artifact it judged. Nothing here was invented from a
theory about what would look good.

**His verdict is always ground truth (R4).** This model is never cited to
overrule him, never cited to argue with a verdict, and never cited to skip a
screening. When the model and the founder disagree, the founder is right by
definition and **the model is wrong and gets amended** — that is the only
direction that correction ever runs.

**Versioning.** v1 is the first fit, taken from the corpus of 2026-07-25 →
2026-08-08. It is iterated on MISSES: every provisional pick logs a prediction
(§3), his ratify/flip logs the outcome, and a miss is a defect in this file, to
be fixed in v2 with the miss cited. A model that is never wrong on the record is
a model nobody is scoring honestly.

**Standing limits this file operates under.**

- It never edits `taste/sapling.founder.v0.3.md` (R4 — the founder's file).
- It authorises nothing. No publication, no spend, no media from unapproved
  script text (§6). A high score is a reason to render a candidate for him to
  look at, never a reason to ship one.
- Every provisional pick made on it is labelled PROVISIONAL on every surface it
  appears on, and lives in provisional/review paths — never overwriting canon
  stills or tracked clips.
- **"A metric agreeing with me is not a sample"** (founder rule, 2026-08-03).
  This file is a metric. It is the thing that rule was written about.

## 1. The axes, ranked

Ranked by how often and how decisively he has ruled on them. Each axis states an
OBSERVABLE test — something a steward or a script can check on the pixels or the
audio — because an axis you cannot check is a mood, not a predictor.

His dominant objection. Four of the five rejections in the 2026-08-08 pass over
forty candidate frames turned on it.

> tall in each clip of it, and thats pretty much it. dont overthink the leafs on

### A3 — In-world plausibility `weight 4`

### A6 — Palette `weight 3`, and it is ASYMMETRIC

The asymmetry is the useful part, and it is the thing a naive colour metric gets
backwards.

**He rejects per-frame drift against neighbours** — A5 above, *"grayened …
drastically changes the style"*; and the v2 pastel look died on being
unreadable, *"i can barely make anything out of it"* (2026-07-27).

**He tolerates a uniform global shift once he has seen it and understood it.**
Shown LTX-2.3's clips on 2026-08-06 he first filed a defect — *"turn black and
white … an unnecessary colour transition"* — and then, the same day with the
measurement in hand (**86-89% chroma loss, Cab 28.07 → 3.78**), ruled it
**"fine"**, and ruled the fp8-vs-bf16 difference **"barely a difference"**
(numerically exact: the two are under 0.5 JND apart).

**Corollary, and it is a real prediction:** a whole-clip or whole-episode grade
change is a weak objection; a single shot that does not match the shot beside it
is a strong one. **Do not weight a global colour metric as if it were a taste
signal.** On 2026-08-08 he mixed rounds across the 7/8/9 progression
(`p07-r1-s2`, `p08-r2-s0`, `p09-r1-s2`) — discarding the palette-locked trio
built specifically to remove cross-shot drift — and the whole-frame mean-RGB
spread that "favoured" his pick was measuring composition, not colour.

## 2. Scoring procedure

### Stage 0 — admissibility gates (binary; a fail means DO NOT SHOW IT TO HIM)

A gate failure is not a low score. It means the candidate is unjudgeable and
putting it in front of him spends his attention on a question he cannot answer.

| gate | fails when |
|---|---|
| **G1 approved plate** | the candidate is staged on, conditioned on, or demonstrated with a still that is REVOKED or was never approved (A10) |
| **G2 §6 script approval** | the beat's script text is open or unapproved — no media may be made from it at all |
| **G3 neutral sheet** | the contact sheet carries a favourite mark, an ordering, a "closest to", or any steward preference |
| **G4 resolvable address** | a candidate has no label resolving through the pass's address key, or its seed is read off a caption instead of its own sidecar |
| **G5 provisional labelling** | the surface does not say PROVISIONAL, or the output path would overwrite canon |

### Stage 1 — score each axis −2 … +2

`+2` exemplary · `+1` fine · `0` neutral/not applicable to this beat · `−1`
noticeable fault · `−2` the fault he has named before in these words.

Score only axes that apply. A2 does not apply to a macro; A7 does not apply to a
beat where the sapling's height is not in frame (episode 2 exempted beats 12,
16, 18, 20, 21 for exactly this reason, with the reason in each sidecar).

### Stage 2 — vetoes before totals

**Any axis at −2 vetoes the candidate as a PICK**, regardless of total. He does
not trade a named fault against a virtue: beat 12 *"follows the style well"* and
was rejected on environment; beat 10 *"actually has character consistency"* and
was rejected on the script.

### Stage 3 — total and rank

`score = Σ (weight_axis × value_axis)` over applicable axes. Rank descending.
Ties break by the highest-ranked applicable axis (A1 first, then A2, A3, …).

### Stage 4 — what gets built from the ranking

1. The top candidate becomes the **PROVISIONAL pick**, labelled as such
   everywhere, into a provisional/review path.
2. Machines render ahead on it. Nothing is promoted to a canon filename, nothing
   is published, nothing is posted.
3. The prediction is written to the ledger (§3) BEFORE he sees anything.
4. He is shown a NEUTRAL sheet — every candidate, addresses and seeds only, no
   favourite. **The provisional pick is disclosed as ours in words, on a
   separate line, so it is cheap to overrule** — never as a mark on the sheet
   (G3).
5. His answer ratifies or flips. Either way it lands in the ledger.

### Standing behaviours the corpus requires, which no score expresses

- **Rejection with no stated axis is legal and complete.** Beat 6: *"none quite
  work"*, no fault named. The correct response is the same words on new seeds.
  **Inventing a fault for him is worse than redrawing.**
- **Delegation back is real and must be recorded as OURS.** *"well, you can
  decide"*, *"you decide"*. The steward's call is logged as the steward's, with
  the reason, so overruling it costs him one word.
- **He answers in addresses.** *"r3-s3 and retire"*, *"b15-r3-s1"*,
  *"po7-r1-s2, po8-r2-s0, po9-r1-s2"*. Every surface must make a pick expressible
  in five characters and resolvable without ambiguity.
- **A metric that agrees with the model is not evidence.** Three times on the
  record a measurement backed a steward pick he then rejected: the motion mean
  (K recipe), the leaf-count fix (scored the episode's best beat), the trio
  colour spread (was measuring composition). When a metric and this model agree,
  that is two guesses, not a confirmation.

## 3. The prediction ledger

**Path:** `taste/steward-model.ledger.yaml` — created by the first provisional
pick, appended to thereafter, never rewritten. One record per provisional pick.

Every record is written **before** he sees the artifact. A record added or edited
after his verdict is not a prediction and is worthless; the ledger's value is
entirely that it can be wrong in public.

```yaml
- id: ep1-b03-r4-provisional          # unique, names the pass
  date: 2026-08-09                    # when the PREDICTION was made
  subject: "node 001 beat 03 — replacement frame"
  kind: still                         # still | clip | vo | cut | recipe
  candidates: [b03-r4-s0, b03-r4-s1, b03-r4-s2, b03-r4-s3]
  address_key: REVIEW-KEY-0809.md     # how the labels resolve
  gates:                              # Stage 0, all must be pass
    G1_approved_plate: pass
    G2_script_approved: pass
    G3_neutral_sheet: pass
    G4_resolvable_address: pass
    G5_provisional_labelled: pass
  pick: b03-r4-s2                     # the PROVISIONAL pick
  scores:                             # Stage 1, only applicable axes
    b03-r4-s2: {A1: 1, A2: 2, A3: 0, A4: 1, A5: 1, A6: 0, A7: 1, A8: 0, A9: 0}
    b03-r4-s0: {A1: 0, A2: -1, A4: 1, A5: 1, A7: 0}
    b03-r4-s1: {A1: -2, A2: 1, A4: 0, A5: 1, A7: -1}
    b03-r4-s3: {A1: 0, A2: 1, A4: -1, A5: 0, A7: 1}
  vetoed: [b03-r4-s1]                 # Stage 2, with the axis that vetoed
  totals: {b03-r4-s2: 27, b03-r4-s3: 8, b03-r4-s0: 5}
  predicted_verdict: ratify           # ratify | flip | reject_all | delegate | refuse_to_judge
  predicted_flip_to: null             # if flip, which candidate
  predicted_objection: null           # if reject_all, the axis and his likely words
  confidence: 0.55                    # 0..1, the steward's honest number
  reasoning: >
    A2 is the whole of his beat-3 note ("make sure it looks like its inside a
    house") and s2 is the only unambiguous domestic interior. A1 is weak across
    all four — same fault as the 08-08 wave — so a reject_all is live, which is
    what holds confidence at 0.55.
  # ---- filled in ONLY after his verdict ----
  founder_verdict: null               # his words, VERBATIM, no paraphrase
  founder_verdict_date: null
  outcome: null                       # hit | miss | partial | unjudged
  miss_axis: null                     # which axis the model got wrong
  model_action: null                  # none | amend_v2 | new_axis | reweight
```

**Scoring the model itself.**

- `hit` — he picked what the model picked, or rejected what the model expected
  him to reject.
- `partial` — right pick, wrong reasoning; or right rejection, wrong axis. A
  partial counts against the axis, not against the pick.
- `miss` — he picked something else, rejected the pick, or refused to judge.
- `unjudged` — he did not answer (superseded, withdrawn, overtaken). Never
  counted as a hit.

**Rolling hit rate is published** on the review surface alongside any provisional
pick, so he can see how much the model has earned before he reads its guess.

**Calibration is scored too.** Predictions grouped by confidence band should hit
at roughly their stated rate. A model that is 0.9-confident and 60% right is
worse than one that is 0.6-confident and 60% right; the first is lying, the
second is honest.

**Misses drive v2, and only misses do.** A v2 requires: the miss records that
motivated it, the axis added/removed/reweighted, and his words as the evidence.
`sapling.founder.v0.3.md` §"Validation" sets the bar the FOUNDER's own file aims
at — *"predicts the author's blind choices on unseen candidates ≥ 90%"*. This
model starts at **zero recorded predictions and makes no claim to any rate**;
until the ledger has entries, a provisional pick is a labelled guess and is
presented as one.

## 4. Open — where the model has no axis

Recorded so nobody mistakes silence for coverage.

- **Beat 6's rejection has no stated axis** — *"none quite work"*, no fault
  named. No axis was invented for him and none should be.
- **Set-level adjacency.** *"looks like fifteen unrelated AI images"* and
  *"beats 7, 8, 9 are basically the same picture"* are both complaints about a
  RUN of shots, not one frame, and A1/A5 only approximate them. The 7/8/9 answer
  was a wide-medium-close progression, which he then picked across rounds — so
  the axis, if it exists, is about reading as one camera moving, not about
  matching pixels.
- **Non-recurring characters.** The goblin's drift is accepted by his own
  reasoning (A1 scope). If it reads badly in an assembled cut, that is a new note
  on a new cut, and this section is where the axis would be added.
- **The framing/crop policy** (12% push-in on a native-still crop) has been
  demonstrated only on plates he had withdrawn, so **it has never been screened**
  and no part of this model claims to predict it.
