**Render speed, revised on real data (16 clips, median 239s, range 232-242):**

| | before | now |
|---|---|---|

| one episode | 5.1h | **2.3h** |

The cause is not the card and not the model — it was **VRAM paging**. With the

and the proof is a controlled pair: same beat 1, same seed, same 20 steps, back to
back — **462s at 480x832 without offload, 240s at 704x1280 with it.** Higher
resolution, twice as fast.

## Fixed overnight

- **The crash.** `farm_worker.sh()` passed `text=True` with no `encoding=`, so
  Windows decoded our own queue file as cp1252 and died on 静 in Wan's negative
  prompt. The queue fails cp1252 at byte 372 → 0x9d, byte-for-byte your
  traceback. The decode happened on subprocess's reader thread where it could not
  propagate, so `.stdout` became `None` and it surfaced inside pyyaml. 23 call
  sites; a test that fails if any text-mode read omits an encoding.
- **Captions were two rows inside the platform chrome band.** `CAPTION_MARGIN`
  was exactly `int(1280*0.22)` = 281, putting the last caption row on y998 — the
  band's first row. Equal to the band where it needed to exceed it. Now +2, and
  the test asserts the real invariant against qa_episode's own constant instead
  of a hardcoded 0.20 the two had drifted apart on.
- **The sidecar now records the prompt.** §7.2 says provenance publishes "model,
  prompt, cost" and it published two of three — which is why diagnosing beat 2
  needed re-running the pipeline to reconstruct a string.
