# Plate twins — v34 motion series geometry, 2026-08-10

Every clip in the v34 motion series was rendered by handing the renderer a raw
832x1216 still, which it resized to 704x1280 without regard to aspect: a 24.4%
vertical stretch (26ece5c). The cut those clips were meant to replace is a
cover-centre crop, so dropping a stretched clip into v35 would jump against
every beat beside it.

A **twin** is the same render with exactly one thing changed — `--init` is fed a
704x1280 plate from `plate_prep.prepare_plate` instead of the raw still. Every
other argument is read out of the source clip's own `.meta.yaml`, the text that
actually rendered. Generated by `pipeline/plate_twins.py`.

## The test

`pipeline/plate_ab_measure.py`. Frame 0 against two reconstructions built from
the clip's own approved still — a naive stretch and a cover-centre crop — by mean
absolute pixel difference, 0-255. Lower is what the frame actually is.

This **ranks nothing**. It answers a closed geometric question with a known right
answer. A clip that passes it can still be a bad clip, and the flow metric that
would rank them is disqualified on this series (131e433: highest score on the
beat where the plant fell over).

`plate vs recomputed cover-crop: MAD 0.0000` on every beat below means the box
ran the policy it claimed to run rather than something nearby.

| 08-ltx-r2-s20260739 (raw init, pair) | 2.75 | 17.17 | STRETCH |
| **08-ltx-r2-plate-s20260739** | 17.22 | **2.83** | **COVER-CROP** |
| 07-ltx-r5-g20-s20260738 (raw init, pair) | 2.76 | 11.88 | STRETCH |
| **07-ltx-r5-g20-plate-s20260738** | 11.81 | **2.69** | **COVER-CROP** |

Exactly inverted, on two different beats with two different stills and two
different guidance values (1.0 and 2.0) — and inverted the right way round: the
plate arms now measure the way the clips already in the v34 cut measure
(1.64 vs crop, 11.68 vs stretch), the raw arms the way the stretched
replacements do.

The `vs crop` figure for a passing twin sits at 2.7-2.8 rather than 0 because a
clip's frame 0 is a diffusion output conditioned on the plate, not a copy of it.
The pairing is what carries the finding, not the absolute value.

## Content

composition, upright and rooted, no invented object; the leaves are simply no
longer squeezed. That was the other half of the question and the reason the b08

**Both beat-08 arms are still nearly frozen across those three frames.** That is
the separate defect from the affirmative lock (879820f: median 1.07 -> 0.10,
frozen 0% -> 66%), it belongs to another lane, and the plate neither caused it
nor fixed it. A geometry fix is not a motion fix.

## Scope, and what is not decided here

All fifteen rounds are twinned, not a chosen few. Which round of a beat belongs
in the v35 cut is R4's open call; 879820f and 655d2c6 both record a later round
being worse than an earlier one, so "latest" is not a safe stand-in for "best".
Twinning every round makes each candidate available in correct geometry and
leaves the choice where it belongs. Priority runs latest-round-first per beat so
an interrupted queue still leaves the likeliest candidates on disk.

Beats covered: 03, 04, 07, 08, 10. **Beat 14 is gated** (26ece5c) and is not here.
