# Ready-to-apply fixes that need a re-voice (or the author)

Everything here comes from the three cold reads of 2026-07-25 (two independent
007a gates at 6/10 and 5/10, plus the first whole-series read). The visual and
bookkeeping faults were fixed the same night in `shots.md` and the headers.
These are the rest, held back for one of two reasons:

- **needs a re-voice** — it changes dialogue, and a script whose lines have
  moved must be re-synthesized or the episode speaks lines that are not in it
  (see cycle-008 defect 2; that is exactly how 007a came to say its closing
  line at beat 5). A trunk re-voice is ~1h of local MPS time.
- **author (R4)** — it is a decision about what the show is, not a defect.

Nothing here is speculative: each line is the reader's own proposal, kept in
their words where possible.

## Needs a re-voice — 007a

1. **Name the cost.** The tree spending two weeks of stored growth is the most
   important fact in the episode and appears only in the `## State change`
   notes. Both readers read beat 20's teal root-light as generic magic and beat
   22's wilt as *tired* rather than *spent*. Add a VO line at beat 20, which is
   currently silent:
   > **VO:** Two weeks of growth, saved up in the dark. All of it. Now.
2. **Give the deadline a setter and a penalty.** Fourteen days imposed by
   nobody, failure costs nothing. One clause at beat 03:
   > **VO:** Fourteen days, the magistrate said, or we're a weed with paperwork.
3. **Make beat 19's callback survivable cold.** The chest-press is opaque and
   the fig is from episode 2. Three words in the goblin's line:
   > **SCAVENGER:** A fig, once — you *threw* it at me — when nobody would give
   > me anything.
   (The shot now holds the dried fig in frame, which is half the fix.)
4. **Recast the closing line as in-episode fact.** Beat 29 introduces "patrol"
   and "assessor" — two institutions never seen — in the last thing the viewer
   hears, and at 29 words it is the densest line in the script over the slowest
   hold:
   > **VO:** Taxes I could pay. A count I could stand still for. A category I
   > could fit inside. …What does *she* want?
5. **Put "shrine" in a line, or cut it from the end card.** The intended win is
   the shrine becoming permanent and the audio never mentions a shrine. One
   word in the magistrate's beat-24 dictation.
6. **Sort out "Shade".** Beat 18's "Shade. Water. Answers." collides with
   SHADE-the-settlement, which is never established in dialogue; one reader read
   it as literal shade. Reorder to "Water. Shade. Answers." and have someone say
   the settlement's name once. Also: "Answers" is unevidenced — the tree only
   speaks in VO that no character reacts to, so *how* anyone gets answers out of
   it is never shown.
7. **Somebody address the goblin by his label.** "SCAVENGER" carries eight of
   nineteen captions and nobody says it aloud in 193 seconds; both readers
   scanned the frame for a scavenger and found a goblin. One address by beat 08
   fixes it — but see the naming decision below, which supersedes this.

## Needs a re-voice — earlier episodes (series read)

8. **004: remove or license the third capability.** 001 establishes "sense and
   grow — that's the whole API" with *move* ruled out on screen; 004's VO says
   "I have been moving water toward his field for three days", which is never
   discovered, never costed, and has no room in the timeline.
9. **005: settle the population count.** The assessor says "Population: two"
   and then "Population three" twenty seconds later. Also decide whether the
   farmer is a citizen of a town he does not live in, having said "tell no one".
10. **004/005: settle the tree's height against the ladder.** Both say
    knee-high one day apart, 006a opens on "the height of a man", and
    `style.md` puts 005 at 1.2 m. No animator can serve both.
11. **Cut the recap line from 005 and 006a.** Four episodes open with the
    literally identical sentence; seven carry a recap of the same fact.
12. **003b → 007a: the protocol's cost.** 003b's engine is that one leaf-tilt
    spends the day's whole reserve; 007a has him rehearsing multi-branch
    choreography on cue for two weeks *while banking* two weeks of growth. The
    reader's fix is the good one: make the rehearsal visibly broke — one leaf,
    late, and branch three dead **because** the budget is being hoarded. The
    goblin's notes get funnier when the tree genuinely cannot comply.

## Author's call (R4) — not steward work

13. **Does the protagonist get a name?** Raised by the 006a gate and now much
    sharper: the only proper name in seven episodes is "Dren", a walk-on guard
    in episode 2. So namelessness is not a rule, it is an omission. The
    reader's proposed fix is the best line in the report — **have the magistrate
    read the keeper's name off the form aloud in 006a**, the exact beat where he
    becomes a line item. A line item should have a name on it.
14. **Does the tree want anything?** The active want from 003b to 006a belongs
    to the goblin (a legal place to exist). The tree is asked questions and
    consents; its climactic act of will in 006a is signing a form. One
    persistent want — "keep the goblin safe" is already half-written in 003b —
    plus one on-screen failure before 007a, is the reader's proposal.
15. **004, 005 and 006a are the same episode three times.** Official with a
    document enters, asks questions, leaf tilts, official writes, card updates,
    smash to black. Giving one of the three a different geometry — someone who
    wants the tree gone, or the tree wanting something and being refused — is a
    structural rewrite, not a patch.
16. **Is "nothing is ever at risk" the intent?** R2's no-villains rule means no
    episode has opposition; the finale's antagonist is weather. Exactly one
    thing is in jeopardy across seven episodes. This may be the show's charm or
    its ceiling, and only the author can say which.
17. **006a's re-armed hook.** 006a ends on "shrines declare *to whom*. Those
    words outlive towns" over the empty ruled line, and 007a never touches it.
    Ending 007a on the dedication — the goblin, pen over the blank line, asking
    the tree what to write while the stranger kneels — would pay one hook,
    re-arm another, and let the tree finally name somebody.

## Entry-point findings (distribution, not scripts)

Vertical video is not watched in order. **004 and 003b are close to unwatchable
cold** — both are the second half of the episode before them, and 004 literally
opens mid-sentence on a ceremony from 003b, so a new viewer's first line is a
callback to something they never heard. 001 and 007a stand alone; 005 stands as
a sketch. Worth knowing before choosing which episode to put money or a link
behind.
