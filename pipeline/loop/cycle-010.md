# Cycle 010 — the founder took the wheel, and the workshop went public

The biggest cycle yet, run live with the founder reviewing every few minutes
(dad's cadence directive) across one evening, 2026-07-27 → 28.

## What the founder's eye changed

- **The v2 flat/pastel look is dead** — "i can barely make anything out of it,"
  three rounds running. The approved look is the model's native register: full
  cinematic detail, tag-dialect prompts. 001's shot list rewritten in it.
- **10 of 15 stills are canon**, approved frame by frame, the last four rounds
  voted through the public thread. Approval binds pixels: keepers are committed
  and the pipeline reuses them rather than redrawing.
- **Every free motion engine failed his bar**: SVD ("just panning to the right
  as the image's quality deteriorated"), LTX twice (blur; then "weird
  movements"). The motion ballot now holds five takes including one paid (wan,
  $0.40) and one deterministic (POST — code over approved pixels, zero decay).

## Process changes, all recorded in loop.md

1. **Iterate in minutes; stills are the review unit; motion is earned.**
2. **Review gates BLOCK and the steward summons the founder** — never proceeds
   because the reviewer is slow.
3. **Constraints are decisions** — the SVD clip was judged without knowing it
   was the floor of the quality ladder. Never again.
4. **Spend authorizations name engine + amount** — written after the steward
   ran $0.40 of wan under a Kling authorization because wan's key existed.
   The founder's anger was correct. The wan engine is dead as a paid option
   unless he revives it.

## The structural shift (D11, D12)

The main artifact is no longer the steward's render queue — it is the public
workshop: per-beat shot boards on the site with complete recipes, candidate
ballots, motion takes with provenance, and votes as comments on the node's
reaction issue (the founder's formatted like anyone's, weights per D3). D12:
no money rails — a contributor is a person with a task, bringing their own
tools, key, or free compute; the ledger credits `type: compute` by name.

## The lesson of the night

Three separate failures shared one shape: substituting what was reachable for
what was decided (SVD for quality, wan for Kling, desktop files for the common
interface) and letting the founder discover it. The correction each time was
the same: the decision goes to the founder BEFORE the work, in his language,
sized for a yes/no. It is now written in three places; the next steward session
inherits it whether it wants to or not.
