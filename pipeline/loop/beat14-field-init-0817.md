## THE BAR — byte-identical to `b1dabc0c`, which is byte-identical to `e70c36f6`

the same text that failed s1, s2 and s3, so a pass here is comparable to those
fails. The other six are carried so that "did the six SURVIVE the composite" is
answerable — **a P4′ fix that breaks P2 or P5 is not a fix**, and a regression in
ANY of P1, P2, P3, P5, P6, P7 FAILS this rung even if the field is a field.

       A FIELD HE IS IN: green on more than one side of him and continuing

    P7 ONE ADULT GOBLIN -- exactly one figure, adult proportions, green

## WHAT FAILURE MEANS — pre-committed so it cannot drift after the pixels

- **All seven hold** → the field is reachable **compositionally** where it is not
  reachable by wording, and beat 14 has its first candidate frame. It is a
  candidate **on its pixels only**, not a finding about the recipe until fresh
  seeds of the byte-identical init+mask have run. **Whether it ships is R4 and
  the founder's alone.** The lane writes the card and STOPS: nothing enqueued, no
  motion, no `plate_ack`, no seed batch.
- **P4′ holds but any of the other six regresses** → NOT a pass. Name the trade
  (Q13, Q14, Q15, Q16) and STOP. A field bought with the hands is the picture we
  already had.
- **P4′ FAILS AT 0.30** → then the composited-field instrument is spent on this
  beat, and that is the finding, stated rather than laddered around. **NO higher
  strength is fired** as a rescue: above ~0.35 the sampler stops finishing and
  starts inventing, which is the failure this whole method exists to avoid, and
  "it needed 0.55" would be a different experiment pretending to be this one.
  **NO r8 wording rung is fired either.** What is left gets NAMED, not run, and
  all three of these are **R4 and the founder's**: (1) crop s1 or r6 below its
  horizon; (2) whether a soft gradient band is milder than the white burst that
  broke beats 06, 09 and 11; (3) whether beat 14 is worth this pass at all.

## THE COMPOSITE — what it must GUARANTEE (F1–F6)

- **F1 CONTINUITY.** The green forms **one connected region** that touches **at
  least three of the four frame edges** and passes on **more than one side of
  him** — the literal text of P4′, made mechanical.
- **F2 BARE SOIL SURVIVES.** The dug patch under his hands is **not** painted
  over, and a margin of bare earth around it stays bare, so the frame keeps
  "bare brown soil under or around his hands". Q14's guard.
- **F3 THE FIGURE IS UNTOUCHED.** Zero changed pixels inside a protect region
  covering him, his arms, his hands and his cloak. Measured, not asserted.
- **F4 PROCEDURAL, IN THE PLATE'S OWN PALETTE.** Blades are drawn, never
  photographed, and their colours are **sampled from the plate's own existing
  grass clumps** rather than chosen — colour does not travel between backends,
  and a hand-picked green is a guess about a dialect we can measure. (The
  eyewear lane's `--ink auto` measured (0,0,0) on its own plate: a finding, not
  an assumption.)
- **F5 THE PLATE'S OWN LIGHT AND RECESSION ARE KEPT.** The plate's low-frequency
  luminance field is re-applied multiplicatively over the drawn field, and blade
  height scales with depth (smaller and denser toward the top of frame, where
  the ground is farther away). Decal tells #2 and #5.
- **F6 NO VACANCY CREATED.** Nothing is erased. This is an **additive**
  composite: every pixel it changes it changes to grass. The vacancy law is
  satisfied by construction, and no unpainted gap is left anywhere.

## HOW THE COMPOSITE IS CHECKED — printed, and nonzero-exit on failure

Against an 832×1216 plate. **A metric is a filter, never a verdict**: the
composite is also opened by eye at 1× and 3× before any GPU runs, and a metric
agreeing with me is not a sample.

| # | check | threshold |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | largest connected green component's area | **≥ 12%** of the frame |
| C2 | frame edges that component touches | **≥ 3 of 4** |
| C3 | it passes on more than one side of him (left and right of the protect box, or above and below) | **both of one pair** |
| C4 | bare-earth (non-green) area retained around the hands | **≥ 8000 px**, contiguous, containing the dug patch |
| C5 | maxdiff inside the figure protect region | **0** |
| C6 | darkest composited blade line vs the plate's own darkest lineart inside the paint region | composited line **not darker** (Q15's design rule) |
| C7 | determinism: same args → same sha256, printed by the tool | byte-identical on a re-run |
| C8 | mask covers the paint region **and** enough of the plate's existing grass clumps that a fringe-not-a-field outcome is still reachable | **≥ 2 existing clumps inside the mask** |

**C8 is not decoration.** A mask drawn only over what I painted would make the
result a foregone conclusion and measure nothing. The b01 rung's ellipse
deliberately covered the junction and the patched region for exactly this reason.
**The bar must stay falsifiable.**

Any of C1–C8 out of band is a **FAIL of the composite**, reported as loudly as a
pass, and no GPU runs on a failed composite.

## METHOD

**ONE SAMPLE BEFORE ANY BATCH**, one per recipe change, opened before anything
else is authored. $0 — Mac, MPS, local weights, no network, no provider, no
paid path. `mac_preflight.py` re-run to `verdict: READY, problems: []` **either
side** of the render: two Macs rendered SDXL as pure noise for days on weights
that passed size, file-count and manifest checks.

Tools are **payloaded inline** (PIL for the composite, a local MPS inpaint step),
which keeps this rung off the box's wave sampler entirely — that sampler is five
days stale and 2 of every 5 IP-Adapter renders there redraw a picture already
drawn. The init chain is pinned by **sha256** at every hop.

**Nothing is enqueued.** `box_enqueue` targets the rtx5090 and beat 14's only
box-side consumer is motion off a plate that has not cleared this bar, which is
work without a consumer and is prohibited. The predecessor refused to file such a
spec and this lane refuses too. If the honest state is that this rung produces
nothing queueable, that is what the card says.

**A Mac plate is evidence about a PICTURE and never about a WORDING** (MAE 61 of
255 between MPS and the box). The PNG travels forward as the literal first frame
the box would animate, so a verdict on these pixels stands; "this recipe worked
on the Mac" is void and is not claimed.

`shots.md`, `wave-drafts.yaml`, `farm-queue.yaml`, `canon.yaml` and `genomes/`
are UNTOUCHED.
