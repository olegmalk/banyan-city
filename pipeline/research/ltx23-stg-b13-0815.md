# STG, the one motion lever a Lightricks author named, fired once: it does not move the hold

Measurement pass, 2026-08-15. **One recipe change, one sample, one matched
control rendered in the same job.** Nothing is scaled off this. $0 — local card,
no provider, two clips of 238 s and 288 s on an otherwise idle 5090.

This finishes lever **L3** of `pipeline/research/ltx23-motion-source.md`
(`dfa87c27`), which the round-trip pass (`ltx23-vae-roundtrip-0815.md`,
`70e3c79a`) took as far as "`stg_scale` is reachable from our call path and we
have never passed it".

---

## The answer

**Spatiotemporal guidance at upstream's own recommended setting for LTX-2.3 —
`stg_scale 1.0`, `spatio_temporal_guidance_blocks [28]` — leaves the hold exactly
where it was.** Same period, same number of distinct pictures, same effective
frame rate:

| | control, STG OFF | STG 1.0 @ block [28] |
|---|---|---|
| hold period | **3** | **3** |
| autocorrelation at that lag | 0.923 | 0.861 |
| trough/peak **depth** | **0.038** | **0.059** |
| distinct pictures in 97 frames | 32.0 | 32.0 |
| **effective fps** | **8.0** | **8.0** |
| terminal freeze (bit-identical frames) | none | none |
| cumulative scale over the clip | 1.000x | **1.036x push-in** |
| mean full-res per-pair MAD | 3.76 | 3.21 |
| 10-frame residual after alignment | 9.87 | 12.34 |
| render time | 238 s | 288 s (+21%) |

The comb is the finding, and it is the same comb in both arms. Full-resolution
per-pair mean absolute difference, frames 40-60, grouped in threes:

```
control  0.37 10.48 0.57 | 1.11 8.40 0.36 | 0.42 10.18 0.66 | 0.52 8.80 0.78 | 0.41 10.07 0.39 ...
STG      0.53  8.89 0.49 | 0.63 6.77 0.39 | 0.42 4.93 0.48 | 0.53 8.01 0.46 | 0.63  9.67 0.52 ...
```

One loud pair every three, its two neighbours at 0.4-0.8 out of 255 — the same
picture twice — in **both** arms. STG did not change the structure; it changed
the values inside it.

So the only motion lever a Lightricks person has ever named for this exact
symptom is now **fired and measured, and it is not our fix.** That is reported as
loudly as a win would have been.

---

## What STG DID change, and why none of it is the figure acting

Three real differences, all of them measured by frame alignment rather than by
eye, because a lane made the eyeball error twice on this beat:

1. **An unrequested 1.036x push-in.** Chained 10-frame alignments read 0.995,
   then 1.005 for every window after — a steady creep toward the camera. The
   control reads 1.000x flat. "zoom, dolly, push in" is in the negative on both
   arms. This is the third clip tonight to show camera motion the negative
   forbids (a bark clip judged this evening shows 1.26x), so **apparent movement
   under STG must not be read as the subject acting.**
2. **A crisper picture.** The STG row of the evidence sheet has harder linework,
   more contrast in the face and more defined grass strokes. That is the quality
   effect STG is advertised for, and it is visible. It is not motion.
3. **More travel per ten frames, in the same three-frame steps.** After the
   global scale and shift are taken out, the 10-frame residual rises from 9.87 to
   12.34 (+25%), while the *frame-to-frame* MAD falls from 3.76 to 3.21. The
   picture gets further over ten frames and still moves in threes. Read the two
   numbers together: **more distance, the same number of pictures.**

**Neither figure rocks.** The prompt is arm 4's cyclical clause word for word —
"He rocks slowly forward and back" — and across nine frames spread over the
whole clip both arms hold the same folded pose. The cyclical-clause negative
reported by the wording lane survives STG.

## The frame-62 wall: absent from this beat entirely

Two bark clips judged tonight are bit-identical from frame 62 to 96. **Neither
arm here contains a single pair of exactly-equal decoded frames**, at any index,
and the last frame with a change above 0.05 MAD is frame 96 of 96 in both. The
full-resolution per-pair MAD by twelfths is flat to the end:

```
control  2.93 4.47 3.77 3.02 4.76 3.99 2.76 3.85 4.22 3.13 3.98 4.19
STG      3.71 4.00 3.19 2.65 3.14 2.88 2.63 3.53 3.87 1.92 3.83 3.19
```

So **the wall is not universal**, and it is a different failure from the hold: a
clip can hold every picture for three frames and still be changing at frame 96.
Beat 13's cyclical arm has the hold and does not have the wall; the bark clips
have the wall. Anyone modelling one mechanism for both should account for that.
This pass says nothing about whether STG moves the wall — it could not, because
the beat it was fired on does not have one.

## The depth caveat, applied

`hold_period` is scale-free and reads a ±3% ripple like a freeze — the VAE round
trip, whose frames provably advance 4 px, reported `period=2, strength=0.90` at
depth **0.971**. So depth travels with every number above. Both arms here sit at
0.038 and 0.059, against b06-DONE 0.181 and b02-FIXED 0.431. **Both are real
holds, and STG's is very slightly shallower.** 0.059 is not a result; it is one
sample's worth of a number that moved 0.02 while the period, the distinct-picture
count and the effective frame rate did not move at all.

## How the arms were kept matched

One crop from an asserted plate sha, **one encode feeding both arms** (so
"identical text conditioning" is the same `.pt` read twice rather than an
assumption about Gemma's determinism), one seed, one job. The two render steps
are generated from a single argv list and the generator asserts that the STG
arm's argv is the control's **plus exactly**
`["--stg-scale","1.0","--stg-blocks","28"]`, with every other difference confined
to `--jobs`, `--task`, `--bench-jsonl` and `--bench-label`. Spec:
`pipeline/jobs/ep2-b13-stg-0815.yaml`.

Recipe, unchanged and not under test: 704x1280, 97 frames, 24 fps, two-stage
8@352x640 + 3@704x1280, distilled sigmas, image-crf 33, guidance 2.0, sequential
offload. Prompts measured on the real Gemma tokenizer before firing: **58 and 38
tokens** of a 1024 limit, 37 words — inside the LTX-2 README's 200-word
guidance, so the ~500-word divergence noted in `dfa87c27` §3.3 is a separate
question and is not confounded with this one.

**Block 28 exists.** The distilled transformer config reads `num_layers: 48`, so
`[28]` is a mid-stack block and not an index past the end that would have made
this a silent null experiment. Checked before the render, not after.

## The default path is unchanged, and this is the proof

`--stg-scale` defaults to `0.0` and `stg_kwargs()` returns an **empty dict**
rather than `stg_scale=0.0` when off, so `pipe()` is called with the arguments
this file has always built. Three independent confirmations:

1. **Static.** The commit that added STG (`369e4101`) is **123 insertions and
   ZERO deletions** in `ltx_i2v.py`. Not one existing line moved.
2. **Tested.** Four new cases in `pipeline/test_pipeline.py` pin that
   `stg_kwargs(0.0, ...)`, `(0, ...)`, `(None, ...)`, `("", ...)` and
   `(-1.0, ...)` all return `{}`; that STG on passes exactly the two upstream
   parameter names; that scale>0 without blocks is refused before any weight
   loads; and that STG enters the kwargs in exactly one place.
3. **Empirical, and this is the one that settles it.** The control arm is a
   re-run of `ep2-b13-shade-cycle-s2-0815` — same plate, same asserted sha, same
   seed 20260815, same prompt, negative and flags — rendered by the **patched**
   renderer. Its published mp4 is

   ```
   42043851da4b246cfcd4e858cda828da992d0d48f15ef10d81d3a99c1ceb445a  13-the-shade-STG-OFF-control.mp4
   ```

   which is **byte-for-byte the sha256 of the clip rendered before the STG code
   existed**. The init png matches too (`210932b7…`). A render that does not ask
   for STG produces the identical file, bit for bit.

Its stdout is unchanged as well: the control's log carries the same
`recipe: offload=sequential quantisation=none (bf16)` line and no STG line, while
the STG arm prints `recipe: STG stg_scale=1 blocks=[28]` and
`STG ON: stg_scale=1 blocks=[28] (one extra transformer forward per step; applies
to both stages)`. The sidecar gains a `stg:` key only when STG ran —
`write_sidecar` drops `None`, so the control's sidecar is the shape it always was.

## What this closes and what it leaves

**Closed.** STG as *the* motion lever on our distilled recipe, at the value
upstream recommends. It is wired, defaulted off, tested, and its one sample is on
record. Nobody needs to re-derive "we have never passed stg_scale" again.

**Not closed, and deliberately not touched by one sample:** whether a *higher*
`stg_scale` (the dev config schedules 4, not 1) or a *different* block list
(the dev config uses `[11,25,35,39]`, `[22,35,39]`, `[28]` at different steps)
behaves differently. Those are further samples, one variable each, and this pass
does not license them — it only shows that the documented starting point does not
move the period. The honest reading of one sample at 1.0/[28] is exactly that,
and no more.

**Also live, from this pass rather than from the brief:** the negative prompt
does not suppress camera motion. Three clips tonight show unrequested push-ins
with "zoom, dolly, push in" in the negative, one of them 1.26x. At guidance 2.0
the negative is active (CFG > 1), so this is not the dead-negative case
documented for guidance 1.0. That is its own one-variable question.
