## Two new gates, and what they caught

**`check_sync.py`** — compares each beat's picture, script and recorded voice. Wired
into `render_local.py`, which now refuses the specific beats that fail (per-beat, not
whole-node, so it cannot earn an `--override`).

Ran it across all sixteen nodes: **21 findings, 20 of them stale VO.** Every unposted
node's recordings predate its current script. That quantifies the 2026-07-25 breach —
that audio is not merely unapproved, it is *unusable*. `005-the-assessor` is worst at
8. It also found that **002b beat 18's recording just ends early**, missing its whole
final sentence, which nobody knew.

**`check_invention.py`** — asks whether the model animated the still or invented
something. The signal is not how much moves but the *shape of the change over time*:
legitimate animation returns toward its opening frame, invention walks away and never
comes back.

Two things I got wrong building it, both caught before they reached this page:

1. First version flagged **six** beats. Four were false: beats 4 and 14 are held
   stills whose push-in my own code drives monotonically — a coded zoom is
   structurally identical to "walks away and never returns". It now skips held
   stills by reading their sidecar. Had I not checked, this report would have
   claimed six beats contained invented content.
2. It measured brightness, so the deliberate lighting ramps (*"the dying screen glow
   drains away"*, *"the warm glow slamming brighter"*) looked like inventions. It now
   measures **edges**, which a glow ramp does not move.

And one thing it **cannot** do, stated plainly: it does not catch beat 11's leaves
changing silhouette between frames. I built a "churn" metric for exactly that and it
failed — beat 11 churns 0.46, and beat 01, whose hands are legitimately hammering a
keyboard, churns 0.53. Same number, opposite meaning. **I left the threshold alone
rather than lower it until my one known case tripped**, because fitting a threshold to
a single label is how a mean got trusted over a median all day yesterday. The column
is reported; the gate does not act on it.
