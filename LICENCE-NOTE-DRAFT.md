## Generated images and footage — what we can and cannot grant

*DRAFT for the founder. Not published. Proposed as a new section appended to
LICENSE-CONTENT.md, and summarised on the site.*

The **pictures** in this repository — every approved still, and the footage animated
from them — were generated by machine-learning models. Two of those models attach
conditions we cannot pass on, and this section says so plainly rather than leaving a
reuser to find out.

**What the models are.** Every still in node 001 was drawn by
`cagliostrolab/animagine-xl-3.1`, licensed **CreativeML Open RAIL++-M**. The motion
is Wan 2.2 (Apache-2.0, no output conditions) or, where a beat holds a still, our own
code. Every clip records its own model, prompt, seed and cost in a sidecar beside it —
§7.2 — so you never have to take this paragraph's word for it.

**What that licence says about output.** Two clauses matter, quoted from the licence
text (SDXL `LICENSE.md`, sha256 `19b6998b…`):

> "Except as set forth herein, **Licensor claims no rights in the Output** You
> generate using the Model."

> "**No use of the output can contravene** any provision as stated in the License."

So nobody owns these frames. But their *use* is bound to that licence's Attachment A,
which forbids a specific list of uses — generating content that harms minors,
unlawful discrimination, disinformation presented as fact, and similar.

**Why that is a problem for a CC BY 4.0 offer.** CC BY promises reusers unrestricted
use, and §2(a)(5)(b) of that licence forbids the licensor from adding conditions on
top. We cannot honestly offer you a freedom we do not hold, and we cannot bolt the
restriction onto CC BY without contradicting it.

**What we do instead.** We keep CC BY 4.0, we rely on what it already says — §5(a):
the licensor "makes no representations or warranties of any kind concerning the
Licensed Material… including warranties of title and non-infringement" — and we tell
you exactly where the limit comes from:

- **Our own contribution** — the scripts, the compositions, the direction, the edit,
  the sound, the code — is CC BY 4.0 and we grant it fully.
- **The generated pixels** carry whatever Attachment A imposes. If your intended use
  is remixing this story, that changes nothing for you. If your intended use is on
  that list, this material is not a way around it.
- **Read it yourself:** <https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md>

**Why we did not simply use a cleaner model.** We looked. Every anime-capable image
model checked — Animagine 3.1 and 4.0, NoobAI-XL, SDXL base, PixArt-XL — is
OpenRAIL-family or unquotable. FLUX.1-schnell is nominally Apache-2.0 but its licence
could not be verified from a primary source, and it is not anime-native regardless.
This is the ecosystem's default, not a corner we cut. The vetting tool is
`pipeline/vet_model.py` and it reports its reasoning; run it yourself.

**This is provisional and reversible.** If a still generator appears whose grant we
can pass on, the stills get re-drawn and this section is deleted. Open as **D15** in
`DECISIONS.md`.
