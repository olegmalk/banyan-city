The lab · banyan.city

The workshop, published raw

Banyan City is a place where stories grow like trees — and Sapling, its first series, branches on what the audience picks. This is the room where the show's tools get tested. Every number here was measured on the machines that make the episodes; nothing is a vendor claim.

Three experiments

Who should draw the show? Four AI artists, same prompts

Four free AI art models drew the same three shots at the same settings: the one the show uses now (Animagine 3.1) against 2026's contenders. The author picks the artist, and the pick changes every future frame.

Measured 2026-07-30 · 24 frames · verdict pending
What does "cheaper" cost? All 15 shots at three quality tiers

Every shot of episode 1 rendered three ways: full quality, a 60-second budget, and a 15-second budget. The finding: cheap settings don't just blur — they redraw the picture. Fine for drafts, useless for a shot that has been approved.

Measured 2026-07-30 · 45 frames · one machine, three settings
The render farm, benchmarked

Household laptops against a rented cloud GPU on identical work. The finding: one gaming laptop matches a datacenter card and beats an Apple-silicon Mac about 13× at this particular job.

Measured 2026-07-29 · 4 machines · same 2 frames each

What the money looks like

$1.24 is the total cash this project has spent, ever — $0.50 of rented cloud GPU (all of it on 2026-07-29, the day the fleet benchmark was measured) and $0.74 of paid video-API tests. Everything rendered on the household machines cost $0. That is one number with one source: it is the sum of all 73 rows of ledger/render-spend.csv, read 2026-07-30. Caps live in code — $8 per command, $20 lifetime (budget.yaml), $5 per evening on the cloud lane.

Why any of this is public

The repository is the product: scripts, renders, votes and receipts are all in the open, and anyone can branch an episode or hand back a better take. If a number here is wrong, it is wrong in public — say so in the reactions thread, or read how the whole loop works.
