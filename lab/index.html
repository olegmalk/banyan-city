🔬 The lab

Experiments behind the show, published raw. Every number here was measured on the machines that make the episodes — nothing is a vendor claim.

Model bake-off — who should draw the show?
Four free AI art models, same prompts, same seeds, same machine: our current one (Animagine 3.1) against 2026's contenders. The author picks the winner; the pick changes every future frame.
What does "cheaper" cost? All 15 shots at three quality tiers
Every shot of episode 1 rendered at full quality, at a 60-second budget, and at a 15-second budget. Finding: cheap settings don't just blur — they redraw. Fine for drafts, useless for approved shots.
The render farm, benchmarked
Family laptops versus a rented cloud GPU on identical work. Finding: one gaming laptop matches a datacenter card and beats an M-series Mac ~13× for this job.

All experiments cost $0 except the cloud tests (~3¢ total, ledgered publicly).
